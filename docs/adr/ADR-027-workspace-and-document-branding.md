# Separate product, workspace and legal document identity

**Accepted — 2026-09-16.** ERPX is the Product Brand in application chrome, while a Holding or Branch may supply a private Workspace Brand for organizational context and printed business documents. The Company remains the legal issuer on every document regardless of the selected Document Brand Mode; a `both` composition is Visual Endorsement only and is never approval evidence or a digital signature.

The Branch mark is derived from the persisted record scope rather than a caller-provided Branch identifier, preventing cross-unit logo substitution. Authorized document readers may choose Holding, Branch, or both for that print, while administrators control defaults and assets. Customer logos are validated, re-encoded and served through authenticated storage; browser Print/Save as PDF embeds the current mark without creating a server-owned PDF record.
