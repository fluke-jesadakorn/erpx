# Product app workspaces and development verification

Accepted for implementation, 2026-10-22. ERPX separates the fifteen products and seven shared hosts into independent Next.js applications in a Bun workspace. A development gateway retains the existing origins and forwards to independently started app processes. Shared owner implementations retain native PostgreSQL transactions; this supersedes ADR-043's single web-runtime topology, without changing Product, Commercial Package, Module or host-bound identity semantics.

Workspace contracts, owner packages and app composition roots replace application-to-application imports. Development verification selects affected dependencies, uses disposable database clones from content-addressed templates and records development-only observations. Full acceptance is exclusively an explicitly invoked pre-production operation: development, PRs, merges and scheduled jobs must not invoke it. Historical receipts remain bound to their original inputs.

The trade-off is additional app/gateway lifecycle management in exchange for smaller compilation and verification scopes. The existing Console compatibility surface remains separately available during migration; a product app cannot delegate business handling to it. Installed production, staging and Commercial activation remain separate operations.

Amendment, 2026-10-23: the user includes development Console cutover and retirement in this delivery. Keep the transitional Console only until every application host passes scoped activation; then the old development Console returns its explicit410 Apps recovery surface (health remains available), with no business forwarding, mutation replay or automatic fallback. This uses development verification, preserves production acceptance as deferred and does not activate Commercial enforcement. Production and staging remain outside this authorization.

Development entry retains current module authority at that cutover: otherwise removing Console would strand existing authorized users who have no Commercial purchase metadata while enforcement is intentionally disabled. Only the exact local/development Console origin with routing, SSO and retirement enabled may defer a product's Commercial admission, and only while that owner's package enforcement remains disabled. Discovery, role selection and direct-page admission continue to use current Company/scope/assignment/version, published IAM permissions, modules, readiness and the normal role-selection CAS. This is no persisted or synthetic Commercial grant; purchased/history admission remains unchanged outside that bounded mode, including shadow/enforced owners. Native business handlers retain their own policies and transactions. Disposable development browser profiles declare and bind retirement separately from compiled acceptance.
