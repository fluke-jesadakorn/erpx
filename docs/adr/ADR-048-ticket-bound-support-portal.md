# Ticket-bound Support Portal

The declared Helpdesk Portal add-on must serve a Company without a Sales purchase. Existing Customer Portal access binds a Sales quotation or order; forcing a general Support issue through that binding would invent a commerce transaction and its customer-party prerequisite. Support therefore owns a distinct independently approved binding from its original ticket to one explicitly selected verified Account email, with a minimal subject/summary publication. The binding preserves the original ticket/customer/source identity and creates no account, customer party, role, grant or Sales order.

The public workflow uses normal central Account/Portal sessions and its own ticket projection, without first acquiring the Sales projection. Approval permits only the reviewed public snapshot, authorized customer-shareable previews and explicitly published Support responses. Public acknowledgement records receipt of a response; original Support assignment, resolution and independent closure remain separate. Field Service-managed tickets retain their original owner and are excluded. Current purchase, release, approved/unexpired binding, verified identity, revocation and private-file guards still apply to every access and retry. Source1065 installation, populated restore and authenticated browser acceptance remain separate from this source decision.
