# Holding addresses and workspace paths

Accepted 2026-10-26 for development. One exact registered hostname identifies one Holding. Applications use paths inside that host: `/{branch}/{app}` and `/{branch}/{department}/{app}`; Company-wide work carries an explicit Company selection. Customer publication and portal services share the Holding address, while Account remains the central identity issuer. Production `{holding}.erpx.com` names are planning metadata only. This supersedes ADR-043's product-origin identity and ADR-050's separate Apps host, while preserving ADR-049's independent applications and all owner transaction boundaries.

An SSO child belongs to the exact Holding host and environment. Company access, organization lineage, package/readiness and original record policies remain independent checks. Former product hosts return generic404 without navigation or mutation forwarding. Development names use registered `{name}.jesadakorn.com` entries; reserved hosts, particularly staging, are never adopted. Conflicting organization route slugs are replaced once under a transactional migration preserving IDs, business history and relationships, then ordinary route immutability is restored. There are no old-slug aliases.
