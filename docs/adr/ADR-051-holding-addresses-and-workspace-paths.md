# ADR-051: Development Holding addresses and workspace paths

Development routing identifies a Holding by one exact registered host and places independently owned applications under scoped paths. Account remains the central issuer. A Holding child session binds to the exact host and environment; Company authority and record policies remain with their owners. Unknown hosts, cross-Holding references, and unregistered product host forwarding fail closed.

A one-time owner migration resolves route-slug collisions without changing node identities or historical records. Work remains a separate product; this decision does not retire it. Production and staging routes are outside this delivery.
