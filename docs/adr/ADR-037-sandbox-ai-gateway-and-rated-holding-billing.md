# Sandbox AI Gateway and rated Holding billing

Accepted 2026-09-28 for Sandbox implementation only. ERPX routes internal AI features through a Holding-local gateway and retains request scope and billable units without copying prompts or responses to Control. A versioned Holding price applies to complete named-seat time, original-file byte-time, and statement-reconciled ERPX-funded AI cost; BYOK, failed work, and simulation do not create AI charges. One Holding receives a month-end invoice only after the entire period is complete and a Platform Admin records an approval reason. The target offering is **THB 499 per active named seat-month from the first seat, with no fixed Holding fee**, 2 decimal GB of original files per seat-month, THB 5 per excess GB-month, and verified AI cost plus 10%. Ten full-month seats receive 20 GB in total. This replaces historical Support only for an explicitly assigned future Sandbox period; old ADR-028 invoices are never recalculated. One user with access to multiple Companies or Branches consumes one Holding seat. Seat time and included storage scale together; missing access history holds the entire invoice.

The target price remains in shadow mode for existing customers until two complete parallel cycles, per-Holding margin evidence, and backup-inclusive storage capacity approval support migration. The 1 GiB per-Company storage cap stays in force until capacity is approved. Stripe test keys and test objects only; automatic card collection requires consent, while PromptPay uses customer-initiated `send_invoice`. Confirmed Stripe outcomes mirror to an isolated ERPX Holding test ledger. No live collection, statutory Thai tax invoice, ordinary Finance posting, or workspace suspension is authorized by this decision.

This deliberately supersedes the *future Sandbox offering* in [ADR-028](ADR-028-sandbox-cost-plus-support-billing.md), while retaining ADR-028's historical Support invoice and review semantics. Stripe owns issued invoice and payment status; ERPX owns meter evidence, rating, approval, and test-ledger reconciliation.
