# Source-bound worker preparation foundation

Projects requires Active Staff before its first Project, while its included worker-reference foundation previously only consumed existing workers. Requiring a full People purchase leaves a Company with no workers unable to start. The development migration therefore extends this foundation with a closed People-owned preparation for an explicitly selected existing Company account, using actual Company HR and Organization authority and the original Directory linking and HR-only placement workflow. It preserves account, Person, Engagement and lifecycle identities; it creates no accounts, access assignments, grants or Project memberships.

This is a deliberate bounded extension, not an assertion that reference consumption already permitted onboarding. Generic People APIs and purchases stay unchanged. The initial constructor proves Projects and its worker-reference foundation, current source/assignment/release and the exact account link; another consumer needs its own declared proof. It retains original branch, Position, Staff Level, manager, CEO, date and version rules. Preparation and placement submission require new-work access. Compensation, Payroll, leave defaults and access recipes are outside this constructor.

An already submitted, source-backed HR-only placement settles existing work while the package drains or expires, under current owner authority. Using new-work at its due tick could strand a reviewed placement because the original cancellation policy refuses overdue cases. The pending placement remains a concrete withdrawal obligation until applied or cancelled. Generic incoming lifecycle jobs retain their original purchase purpose. This decision records implementation scope; source, native, installed and browser acceptance remain separate gates in the [production plan](../production-plan.md).

Learning now declares the same bounded constructor for its included learner reference: an authorized Company with no active workers can prepare its selected existing account before original course assignment. It uses Learning and learner-reference purchase/readiness plus current HR/Organization authority, with distinct constructor and placement provenance. Projects provenance and purchase do not substitute for this proof. Shared private execution preserves original People identities and HR rules; generic People access, account provisioning, compensation, leave and Project membership remain independent. Source1064 and its jobs/withdrawal proof require their own installation and acceptance.
