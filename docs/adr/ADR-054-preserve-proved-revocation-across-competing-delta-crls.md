# Preserve proved revocation across competing delta CRLs

Accepted 2026-10-27 for the native PDF verifier migration. Independently authenticated and applicable revocation evidence must not disappear because a non-revoking delta CRL occurs first in the supplied materials. Preserve cryptographic integrity, strict chain, revocation and diagnostic outcomes separately; conflicting or unsupported evidence cannot establish `valid` by ordering alone.

The frozen synthetic `competing_delta_revoked_second` case is retained at commit `b4db260cd029707886e82ac958daab3b4fab1509` in the [CRL guard corpus](../../fixtures/legal/pdf-signatures/conformance-crl-guards.v1.json). Its PDF SHA-256 is `6a88ea749e0ca46a040d79f56240412c7bf5c7b2ca8d0bc2667f7243088126d9`; the canonical sorted case SHA-256 is `cf6714dfba9e4e71bf497bc7086e5356b9b5b2b18b6fe0d03b38668ab06c545d`. The B0 oracle reports `valid` when its first matching delta omits the revocation while a later signed applicable delta proves it. Native verification reports integrity `valid`, revocation `invalid` and overall `invalid`. This deliberately closes that legacy oracle gap; it does not mislabel sound signature mathematics as invalid integrity.

Preserve the actual B0 result and material hashes unchanged.

The native policy evaluates authenticated applicability, freshness, issuer, distribution-point scope and reason coverage before treating a CRL as evidence, following the processing concerns in [RFC 5280 section 6.3.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5280.html#section-6.3.3). A supplied untrusted, stale, unrelated or unsupported CRL cannot on its own assert revocation. Ambiguity without an independent applicable proof remains `indeterminate`; a positive revocation proof is not hidden by another good delta or OCSP response. This is an explicit conservative migration decision, not a claim that the RFC prescribes this exact competing-material selection policy.
