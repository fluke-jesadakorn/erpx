# Thai payroll source review — 2026-10-02

This engineering review enables the dated `thai-payroll-v1` adapter for ordinary monthly employment income under Revenue Code section 40(1) and the ordinary Social Security section 33 contribution schedule. It does not certify a payroll provider, execute a bank transfer, or establish support for termination lump sums, a special reduced contribution rate, or another statutory category. Independent internal approval of the source revision and each employee's allowance/YTD evidence remains required.

## Primary evidence and effective dates

The Revenue Department's [order ป.96/2543](https://www.rd.go.th/3558.html) specifies annualising ordinary recurring employment income by expected payments, subtracting the applicable expenses and allowances, calculating annual tax, and allocating ordinary withholding across payments. Its examples treat a one-off bonus as one payment and calculate overtime withholding as the incremental annual tax on cumulative overtime. The order also describes the December actual-income reconciliation and the final employment month's reconciliation. The adapter therefore retains prior taxable income, irregular income and tax withheld separately; it does not multiply a one-off bonus by twelve.

The [Revenue Code sections 42 bis and 47](https://www.rd.go.th/5937.html) provide the 50% employment expense deduction capped at THB 100,000 and the THB 60,000 personal allowance. Other declared annual deductions must be independently reviewed and evidenced in the employee's immutable allowance profile; the engine does not infer eligibility for family or investment allowances. This profile's verified annual deductions include applicable declared SSO deductions, so that contributions are not deducted twice.

The [Revenue Department's income tax rate schedule](https://www.rd.go.th/5938.html) supplies the graduated bands applicable from tax year 2017. The [THB 150,000 net-income exemption](https://www.rd.go.th/31847.html) is represented as the first zero-rate band; subsequent bands are 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, and 35%. The adapter's reviewed revision covers calendar 2026 only. A later tax year requires a separately reviewed revision even when these constants appear unchanged.

The enacted [Social Security wage-base regulation, Gazette volume 142, part 81 ก, pages 5–6, dated 12 December 2025](https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/98728.pdf) takes effect on 1 January 2026. It sets the monthly minimum wage base at THB 1,650 and the ceiling at THB 17,500 for 2026–2028, THB 20,000 for 2029–2031, and THB 23,000 from 2032. The regulation was located through the [Department of Employment's official information centre](https://infocenter.oic.go.th/doe/topic.php?gid=86775&mid=1017&p=1), downloaded, and visually inspected as a two-page Gazette document; the adapter uses the 2026–2028 ceiling only.

The Cabinet's [2 December 2025 official briefing](https://www.thaigov.go.th/uploads/document/74/2025/12/pdf/20251203161231_8203.pdf) describes the ordinary 5% employee/employer rate and the resulting maximum THB 875 contribution. [Chulalongkorn University's employer HR instructions](https://www.hrm.chula.ac.th/th/home/social-security-fund) document its operational section 33 calculation, including whole-baht rounding and minimum THB 83. The adapter uses 500 basis points per party with half-up whole-baht rounding. A generic rate must not silently override a separately applicable temporary or regional relief measure; this revision is explicitly the ordinary section 33 schedule.

## Paid corrections and reviewed recovery — 2026-10-03

The [Revenue Department order ป.96/2543, clause 1(5)](https://www.rd.go.th/3558.html) calculates occasional earnings using the incremental annual tax and includes ordinary withholding when ordinary wages are paid in that occurrence. The additional-pay adapter retains the original ordinary basis, pays only the approved arrears/omitted OT and charges the incremental tax. Its December result reconciles actual retained income and withholding. Original posted wages, paid bank instructions and issued slips remain immutable. Contribution differences use the retained wage-period contribution and prior posted adjustments, preventing the monthly ceiling from being charged twice.

The Ministry's [Labour Protection Act, sections 76–77, PDF page 27](https://www.mol.go.th/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/1998/01/Labour_Protection_Act_BE2541.pdf) restricts deduction categories, gives default 10% individual/20% aggregate limits for specified nonstatutory deductions and requires a specific written agreement. Consent does not automatically classify every employer debt as permissible. ERPX requires the original employee's specific agreement, a separate recorded permissibility review, and separate Finance recognition. Its internal dated policy keeps the conservative limits even with consent; income-tax/SSO refunds remain separately reviewed.
