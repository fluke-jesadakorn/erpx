Business behavior:
- A creator or project owner can delegate a non-terminal task to one active staff member for up to 90 days with a required reason.
- The task version increments and the command writes an immutable audit event, outbox event, notification and delegation record atomically.
- The active delegate becomes the current Flow work owner and can start, save progress, complete and upload evidence while the delegation is active.
- Revoke uses the task version and idempotency receipt; expiration or revoke immediately removes task visibility/action access for the delegate and returns work ownership to the original assignee.
- A second overlapping active delegation is rejected, and terminal tasks cannot be delegated.
