ERPX Release 1 Finance — controlled period-end FX revaluation
Observed: 2026-09-21 Asia/Bangkok
Environment: isolated PostgreSQL integration databases plus local development database

Delivered behavior
- Company Finance prepares closing rates per foreign currency, selects functional-currency other-income and other-expense accounts, and records preparation proof.
- The preparation stores each invoice's transaction outstanding, prior functional carrying amount, revalued amount, adjustment, rate, version, and a deterministic source hash.
- The preparer cannot approve the run. An independent Finance approver can reject it or approve it into a balanced posted journal.
- Approval rejects changed foreign exposure, a closed period, stale version, duplicate posted period/chart, invalid accounts, and non-period-end dates.
- Approval updates each open invoice's functional carrying balance atomically with the journal and decision evidence.
- Period close includes posted revaluation adjustments in AR/AP source balances and keeps unrevalued open foreign monetary items as explicit blockers.
