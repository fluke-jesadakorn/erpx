Implemented behavior
- Opening trial-balance lines identify receivable and payable control schedules.
- Finance account control_kind distinguishes AR/AP from cash, inventory, tax and other control accounts.
- Each schedule item requires the correct active customer/supplier, matching functional currency, unique reference, due date, control account, offset account and positive amount.
- Each configured AR/AP control-account schedule must equal its GL opening balance exactly.
- Preview tokens bind the company, chart, period, accounts, parties, normalized balances and schedules.
- Import creates the draft opening journal and draft operational Finance invoices atomically under one idempotent command.
- A different Finance approver posts the journal and all linked opening invoices together; posted items appear in normal aging and can use normal receipt/payment allocation.
- Reversal voids unallocated opening invoices with the GL reversal. Any historical allocation blocks direct opening-journal reversal.
- Opening-import invoices cannot be posted separately and therefore cannot duplicate GL.
