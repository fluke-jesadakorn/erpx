## What the new runs verify

- **POS-02:** Restaurant transfer remains unsettled until Finance matches the imported synthetic bank row. The same flow charges a guest folio, receives payment, checks out the guest, refunds a separate cash sale through independent manager approval, and verifies folio/posting/stock/payment counts. Wrong stay and stale sale version requests return errors without changing the sale, fulfillment or stock. Replaying the successful room charge with its original idempotency key returns the same result and does not add another folio source, posting or stock movement.
- **HR-01:** A newly invited H002 employee activates and signs in; cross-holding directory/detail reads fail; after an owner revokes the employee role, reads fail and the employee returns to the access-required route after reload.
- **PAY-02:** Independent review/correction, review export, GL posting, payment export, synthetic bank import and payment confirmation replay complete; one statement/transaction and one settled batch/payment remain. The UI explicitly keeps live employee payslips locked pending statutory calculations and restricted delivery validation. This is accepted as the mock `review export` path; it does not claim an employee payslip was issued.
- **AI-02:** Night Audit assistant output cites current hotel evidence and is hidden after the source changes. When AI is disabled, the operator closes the day manually; the worker advances the business date, and replay returns the same completed job without adding a night record.
- **AUTH-01:** H002 rejects stale/wrong-stay POS commands, self-approval in purchasing/payroll, cross-holding people reads, revoked employee access and a worker acting on a revoked Night Auditor assignment. Rejected commands are checked against database state.
- **MONEY-01:** POS payment, room charge, bank import, supplier partial receipt/settlement, payroll bank confirmation and Night Audit work are replayed. The worker blocks after its actor assignment is revoked; an authorized owner resolves the blocked day and a retry completes once. The test verifies one invoice, balanced journal totals, and no duplicate folio, payment, stock movement or completed night record.
- **UI-01:** Hotel, POS, purchasing, HR, payroll, AI and blocked-night recovery browser paths passed on desktop and Pixel 7 mobile with reload/access/error behavior where applicable.

The recovery test exposed a real UI gap: Front hid every page while a hotel was closing, including the blocked Night Audit row's `Resolve and continue` action. The Front PMS workspace now allows that recovery action only on Night Audit, only for `pms.audit`, and only when the closing property has a blocked night record. The recovery run passed on desktop and mobile after this change.
