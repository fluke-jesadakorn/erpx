Delivered behavior
- A Finance preparer can allocate a positive freight, duty or handling amount to a posted purchase receipt by receipt value or received quantity.
- Item and lot/serial allocation evidence stores stock quantity, value and version snapshots and cannot be updated or deleted.
- A separate Company Finance approver must approve before posting; the preparer and their linked identities cannot self-approve.
- Posting revalidates stock and lot snapshots, increases aggregate and lot inventory value by the same amount, and posts a balanced inventory debit and selected clearing/expense credit.
- Creation, approval and posting stop if stock has left the receiving warehouse after the source receipt, so an unsupported retrospective COGS distortion cannot be introduced.
- Submitted and approved records appear in Work with the responsible queue and next action; status changes notify the preparer.
- Purchase-order and landed-cost detail screens expose creation, allocation, actions, source receipt, journal and immutable event history.
