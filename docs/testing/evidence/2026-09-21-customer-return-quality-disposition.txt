Delivered behavior
- A customer return line records the observed condition (unopened, damaged or unknown), a condition note and a Quality review due date.
- Independent Commerce approval restores the exact returned quantity and value, preserves the original lot/serial allocation, reverses stock/COGS, prepares the credit note, and creates a quantity-specific Quality hold in the same transaction.
- Returned stock remains on hand for accounting but is excluded from available quantity, new reservations and issues until Quality finishes the disposition.
- An independent inspector can record a pass or fail using the existing structured measurements and private evidence workflow. A third Quality manager releases passed stock. Failed stock follows the existing controlled scrap handoff to independent Finance, inventory count evidence and balanced GL.
- Whole-stock holds remain whole-stock controls. Receipt and customer-return holds use partial quantity scope, so unrelated released stock remains available.
- Condition, review date and hold source linkage are immutable. The order page links every customer return to its Quality disposition and status.
