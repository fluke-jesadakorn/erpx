ERPX Release 1 periodic access review evidence
Observed: 2026-09-21 22:04 Asia/Bangkok

Implementation
- Migration 0258 adds access review campaigns, assignment snapshots and immutable review events.
- A company IAM reviewer starts a named campaign with a due date and business reason.
- The campaign captures every currently active assignment with user, role, scope, source, expiry and assignment version.
- The campaign creator cannot certify the campaign and a reviewer cannot certify their own Person/assignment.
- Each item supports keep or immediate revoke with a required reason and fresh MFA.
- Revoke uses the existing protected revocation service, invalidates the selected work context and preserves last-administrator/recovery safeguards.
- Every decision increments the campaign version; changed or revoked source assignments fail closed and require a fresh campaign.
- An untouched campaign can be cancelled by its creator. A campaign with a recorded decision cannot be cancelled.
- The campaign completes automatically when no pending assignment remains.
- IAM command receipts, IAM audit/outbox and immutable campaign events preserve evidence and idempotent replay.
