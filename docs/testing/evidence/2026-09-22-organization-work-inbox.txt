Implemented

- Work, Inbox and organization review evidence recheck the selected Company grant, current permission, assignment/parent validity, principal state and Company workspace boundary. Expired/revoked access is hidden immediately on fresh reads.
- Review eligibility also checks current database person linkage; stale context aliases cannot review their own person's request. The transaction asserts independent identity again.
