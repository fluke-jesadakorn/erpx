ERPX Finance generic controlled payments — 2026-09-21

Implemented scope
- A Company Finance preparer creates a receipt or payment against an active customer/supplier and configured bank control account.
- An independent Finance approver records approval evidence.
- A third Treasury user releases the payment; the preparer and approver are both prohibited from acting as payer.
- The same payer confirms an imported exact bank transaction and allocates the full amount across one or more compatible open invoices.
- Confirmation atomically creates and posts the balanced bank/AR/AP journal, inserts invoice allocations, updates every invoice balance/status, settles the payment, matches the bank row, records decision evidence, audit and outbox data.
- Idempotent retry returns the committed result. Version and transactional assertions protect the payment, bank row, invoice balances, period and posting accounts.
- Refunds remain in the approved return/refund owner workflow instead of bypassing that authority chain.
