## Implemented in this slice

- POS `sale.charge_to_room` handles a full unpaid restaurant bill in the existing POS transaction. It requires a same-company, same-branch, same-currency open hotel, matching business date, in-house stay, open folio, current sale and hotel versions, active cashier assignment, approved PMS accounting, and an open period. Stock and POS cost consumption, the PMS folio entry, and the PMS revenue journal commit together. POS does not create a duplicate revenue journal or a POS payment. A repeated command key returns the original result. The production command is disabled by default until explicit pilot activation.
- A different Finance/POS/PMS approver can now reverse the full linked room charge from the POS bill. The command checks both record versions, reverses the PMS folio and revenue, classifies consumed cost as waste or restores selected resalable stock at original cost, and records one linked correction in a single transaction. Direct PMS reversal of a POS-sourced charge is blocked. The POS sale report excludes reversed room charges; receipt and bill detail identify the adjustment. Partial room-charge refunds and post-checkout settlement corrections still need business acceptance.
- A typed POS bank-transfer reference remains pending. Finance can settle it only by independently matching an imported bank row with the exact bank account, reference, date, currency, and amount. The match and receipt post in one transaction. An unverified reference can be cancelled and the bill recovered. Production POS tender creation allows cash and verified transfer; EDC, Stripe card, and Stripe PromptPay are gated.
- POS exposes limited checked-in stay details to the cashier and shows the linked folio entry after completion. The production workspace now offers only the enabled cash/transfer tenders and hides the room-charge action until the server pilot gate is enabled. An unconfigured POS keeps the setup checklist visible without rendering the long disabled product catalogue.
- Public online booking pages and public booking API are disabled in production pending separate acceptance. The development and isolated demo routes remain available.
- The authenticated, searchable **Future features** page separates deferred items and prototypes from daily work, with a prerequisite for each. The static POS capability support map now reports partial pilot delivery rather than broad live status.
- Completed POS sales and closed PMS folios now show an **External tax document** card. A cashier records the issuer, external reference, issue date, document total, document tax and evidence note against the posted Finance invoice; a different Finance approver checks those independently entered figures against the invoice before verification. Older references with no separately entered document figures cannot be verified and must be corrected. The database permits one active reference per invoice and one active issuer/reference pair per company. A void keeps the prior record and allows a replacement. Room-charged restaurant bills have no separate POS invoice, so their reference belongs to the eventual PMS folio invoice. Printed POS receipts explicitly say they are operational receipts, not tax invoices. This records an externally issued document; it does not issue a Thai tax invoice or approve tax configuration.
- The Finance invoice detail also shows the same reference card, allowing a Finance approver without POS cashier access to verify the recorded external document. A fresh isolated browser run passed all four hotel scenarios: six role workspaces and stock/GL isolation; a kitchen-to-room charge; pending transfer recovery; and a direct cash sale with a fictional document reference, independent Finance verification, and persistent receipt state.
- `demo:prepare` now creates a separate Riverside Lantern holding in disposable `H002` databases and private files, ten independent role accounts, eight rooms, two room types/rates, four inspected rooms, one checked-in example stay, a configured restaurant register/tables/menu, and fictional opening stock. A different Finance user reviews and posts the matching opening inventory journal. The demo's general-company holding stays in `H001`; an unauthorized hotel cashier cannot read its PMS API. The fixture guide includes role-specific steps, expected results, and reset commands. An isolated browser run loaded six independent role workspaces and completed a cashier/kitchen room charge through the real UI; the POS sale, folio entry, and balanced PMS posting remained linked after reload, with no duplicate POS revenue or payment. A separate browser case showed that an unverified transfer cannot complete a bill and can be cancelled safely. The POS remaining-balance display now counts settled payments only.
