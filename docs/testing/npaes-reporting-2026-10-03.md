# NPAEs presentation evidence — checked 2026-10-03

TFAC's current NPAEs index lists the revised 2022 standard for accounting periods beginning on or after 2023-01-01. Its index was updated on 2026-06-09; eligibility remains a Company accounting-policy decision. [TFAC official NPAEs index](https://acpro-std.tfac.or.th/standard/2/-NPAEs).

Chapter 4 requires financial position, income or comprehensive income, changes in equity, and notes (§4.1), with comparable preceding-period information (§4.3). Cash flows are optional under this standard; entities choosing to present them follow TAS 7 (§4.2). Notes identify the reporting basis, selected policies, relevant required disclosures and other material information (§4.19). [TFAC revised 2022 standard, chapter 4, pages 13–21](https://acpro-std.tfac.or.th/test_std/uploads/files/%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%90%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99%20NPAEs/TFRS%20for%20NPAEs_Revise%202565_%E0%B8%89%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%9E%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%A7%E0%B9%87%E0%B8%9B(1).pdf).

ERPX's engineering implementation uses an independently approved, effective-dated NPAEs accounting profile; explicit account presentation; and Company-prepared note disclosures. It captures posted journal/line versions, the original policy revision and a source fingerprint for each draft. An independent Finance approver issues a retained HTML statement with a checksum. Later source changes require a new edition linked to the earlier issued copy. Equity reconciles opening balances, owner movements and the period result to the posted ledger.

The fiscal-close adapter transfers actual income/expense balances to a selected retained earnings account only after earlier periods have closed. It keeps the original transfer and prevents new backdated journal drafts/postings after a reviewed year is closed. Independently reopening an accounting period preserves the fiscal transfer; subsequent financial adjustments belong in an open year. This is an internal engineering control, not a TFAC certification claim.

`tests/integration/finance/finance-reporting.test.ts` uses ordinary owner commands, separate preparer/approver identities and synthetic Company transactions. It checks real nonzero AR/AP subledgers against GL, two consecutive monthly closes, comparative/current income, owner distributions, equity, immutable issued/amended exports, fiscal transfer, and stale-source/scope failures. Optional assertion-emitted `financial-continuity.json` records the exact source/build identity supplied by the release verification runner. Stock/WIP equality is separately proven by the Manufacturing and Commerce owner tests.
