# Expense workflow backlog

## P0 — operational continuity

1. Delegation and out-of-office windows with scoped delegates, start/end times, separation-of-duties validation, and complete audit history.
2. SLA timers and reminders for unclaimed and overdue department, Accounting, Executive, Finance, and settlement tasks.
3. Budget availability checks at submission and approval, with reservation and release on rejection.
4. Duplicate receipt and payment detection using document hashes, amount/date/vendor similarity, and bank references.

## P1 — accounting automation

1. VAT eligibility and withholding-tax suggestions with human confirmation and generated certificates.
2. Bank reconciliation that matches confirmed payments to imported bank statement lines.
3. Fraud and anomaly scoring for altered receipts, unusual vendors, split claims, repeated references, and policy outliers.
4. Configurable COA mapping rules by vendor, department, expense category, and tax treatment.

## P2 — advanced settlement

1. Partial and installment payments with remaining-balance controls and per-payment settlement journals.
2. Foreign-currency claims with document currency, approved exchange-rate source, realized FX differences, and THB reporting.
3. Supplier master validation and payment-detail change approvals.
4. Cash-advance clearing and employee recovery flows.
