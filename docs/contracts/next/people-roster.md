# People roster

People owns planned workforce scheduling (ADR-008 amendment). POS owns cash sessions. A roster is neither attendance evidence nor a payroll input.

## Screens and scope

`{workspaceBase}/people/roster` works in Holding, Branch, and Department workspaces. `department` selects an authorized department; `week` is its Monday date; `view=requests` and `request` deep-link from the inbox. Staff see published weeks in their active engagement's department. Roster managers can also see drafts within their selected workspace scope. Department scopes include descendant departments, while requests only exchange work within the exact same department and branch.

My shifts and shift requests are available to active staff whose Position has `rosterRequired=true`. Backoffice staff do not need a Roster or Open Slot, even if their Business Role grants `self.roster`. Authorized backoffice managers can still manage their team's Roster. Authorized managers also see Shift templates and create, copy, publish, assign, edit, and cancel controls. Desktop uses a weekly grid; mobile uses daily cards. A published change requires a reason. Already-started shifts cannot be changed. Published weeks cannot be unpublished, and their timezone is fixed at creation. Templates are copied into shifts, never applied retroactively.

## API and records

`GET /api/v1{workspaceBase}/people/roster?department=…&week=…` returns `RosterData` with permitted departments, the selected week, nearby weeks, shifts, assignments, request actions, conflicts, and history. Drafts and manager-only data are removed server-side. Requests retain their original assignment references even after an exchange. The calendar query includes five weeks before and after the chosen week; request details additionally include their referenced shifts.

`POST` to the same URL accepts the discriminated `RosterCommand` in `src/lib/people_payroll/people-roster.ts`. The actions are `template.save`, `week.create` (optional `copyWeekId`), `week.publish`, `shift.save`, `shift.cancel`, `request.create`, and `request.decide`. Writes require an `Idempotency-Key`; edits and decisions include `expectedVersion`. Shift saves check the containing week's version, and shift cancellations check the shift's version. Responses use the existing `{data}` / `{error:{code,message}}` convention. Reads are private, uncached, and do not create notifications.

The additive holding-write migration `0035_people_roster.sql` defines templates, weeks, shifts, assignments, requests, append-only history, and a company mutation revision. It also registers permissions and adds immutable built-in role revisions. Existing POS records and commands are unchanged.

## Workflow and invariants

- Extra slot: pending manager → approved / rejected / withdrawn.
- Move: pending manager → approved / rejected / withdrawn. Approval releases the original assignment and creates its replacement in one transaction.
- Swap: pending coworker → pending manager → approved. Coworkers can reject before accepting; managers can reject after acceptance; requesters can withdraw before approval.
- Neither participant can be the final reviewer, including through another linked principal of the same Person. Changing referenced shifts or assignments invalidates pending requests and notifies their participants.
- Owners, HR admins, People managers, and Operations managers receive `roster.read`, `roster.manage`, and `roster.approve` in their allowed scopes. `self.roster` is identity-based and additionally requires an active staff engagement and account. Custom roles can receive the scoped permissions through normal IAM administration.
- All shifts use an explicit IANA timezone, default Asia/Bangkok. Monday–Sunday refers to local shift start dates. End time earlier than start means the next day; equal times are rejected. PostgreSQL resolves local times; nonexistent daylight-saving times are rejected by round-trip validation. Ambiguous times use PostgreSQL's timezone resolution consistently.
- Assignment validation checks active staff and account eligibility, placement, engagement dates, approved leave, overlaps (including overnight and adjacent weeks), and capacity. Validation repeats at publication, coworker acceptance, and final approval. Approved leave introduced later appears as a coverage conflict without silently cancelling the published assignment.
- Every mutation, its history, audit/outbox event, receipt, and notifications commit together. A company roster revision serializes mutations across weeks; SERIALIZABLE retries recompute checks after concurrency conflicts. Shift/assignment row locks protect affected records. Two managers cannot fill the last slot twice.
- Assignment, copied assignments, publication, and Open Slot approvals recheck the occupant's active roster-required Position. Publishing checks each active employee or contractor in the department who works during the Monday–Sunday week, including staff awaiting an account link. A midweek start is included; a weekend-only partial first week is included too. Approved leave covering all working days excuses the person. Managers see missing coverage for this week and next week; scheduled jobs send deduplicated manager notices. Moving to a backoffice Position releases only future assignments, invalidates related pending requests, and records history and notices with the HR change.
- Copying a week creates independent draft shifts and assignments; it never copies requests or decisions. It validates all proposed assignments and rolls back the entire copy on a conflict.

## Development examples and verification

Run `bun run db:migrate:local`, then `bun run seed:roster:local`. Seeding requires local `erpx_dev_*` databases and existing fictional Acme organization profiles. It adds no accounts, grants, or passwords. It seeds Bangkok Operations and Warehouse with three shift templates, a published current week when future shifts fit, a published next week, vacancies, and pending-coworker/pending-manager/approved/rejected requests. When the current week has no remaining schedulable days, it reports the gap and does not create past shifts. Stable command receipts resume interrupted work; completed examples and pre-existing weeks are preserved on rerun.

`tests/integration/people_payroll/people-roster.test.ts` exercises publication, private drafts, copy snapshots, request lifecycle, independent approvals, scope, overnight intervals, leave/engagement conflicts, invalidation, replay, and concurrent capacity/overlap checks. POS and navigation regression tests must also pass. Use the pinned runtime for typecheck, lint, tests, and isolated release builds; keep dev on port 3100 and verify the public hostname with an authenticated browser.

## Development product purchase boundary

`ERPX_PEOPLE_PACKAGE_ENFORCEMENT` is local-development-only and defaults to `disabled`; a grant never enables it. Roster history and notification destinations require `people.workforce`, current assignment/IAM and the original scope and record policies. Creating/copying/publishing weeks, assigning/editing shifts, templates and new requests need active work. Existing request decisions, shift cancellation and Calendar rescheduling retain settlement while draining, including commercial expiry. Exact authorized command replay uses retained history. Projects/Learning worker/learner references do not acquire a full People dependency from this Roster boundary.

Commercial fences surround the original Roster or Calendar Holding transaction. Deferred owner effects finish before the final current-authority and purchase checks, retaining receipts, audit, history and notifications atomically. Scheduled coverage takes Holding purchase fences before its source snapshot, skips unpurchased Companies, monitors draining work and keeps the original live recipient authority checks. Server content includes only optional minimal purchase booleans/revision; disabled response shape is unchanged. Bounded Roster/Calendar native tests cover late clock and post-receipt revocation rollback. Generic Work/search/Files projections, concrete People withdrawal obligations, package-only browser and whole-system acceptance remain required.

Calendar's actual Roster source projection applies retained workforce history and current authority before its row limit, using one database clock. Its occupant query uses the same source decision. Unpurchased Roster contributes neither shifts nor occupant names while other authorized Calendar sources remain visible. Draining retains existing rescheduling but removes new planning controls. A populated native case covers no purchase, People base without workforce, active and draining access alongside an ordinary appointment; this adds one case to the existing Calendar suite rather than re-certifying the complete Roster cohort.

Template, week, shift, request and review drawers now protect unsaved work on keyboard/close/navigation. Valid parsed acknowledgements clear only the submitted editor revision; later edits remain, saved template/shift identities rebase without remounting the newer fields, and uncertain retries retain the original payload/key/role context. Stale responses do not replace a newer snapshot. Actual Console browser exposed a template lost on Escape; the fixed drawer now displays Keep editing/Discard draft, retains the exact typed value and resets only after explicit discard. No roster command was submitted during that browser check. Bounded UI tests cover those transport/draft transitions; installed enforcement and the full responsive/theme/widget matrix are still unaccepted.
