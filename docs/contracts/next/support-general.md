# General and employee Support reports

The Support page accepts a direct issue report with a title and description. It does not require a sales order, customer, owner or follow-up date. The report enters `triage`; a Support manager assigns an eligible Support colleague and a follow-up date before work begins. The assigned colleague records progress and a proposed resolution. A different Support manager confirms a general issue's outcome with a reason or returns it for more work. Immutable events, optimistic versions, idempotency and current scope/recipient guards apply to each command. An unassigned report can be cancelled by a Support manager.

The existing sales-linked Support path remains separate. It still validates the quotation or sales order and current source version, snapshots the customer, and requires a recorded customer contact after resolution for independent closure. A general report cannot be confirmed with a customer contact note. General reports are excluded from customer-order access sessions, shared customer incidents and field-service source selection until their own source and access rules are implemented.

Support files have two non-interchangeable audiences. `internal_evidence` follows the current assigned colleague/eligible Support-manager boundary. `customer_shareable` requires a Support manager and becomes visible only as a sanitized preview to the authenticated customer bound to the same approved order. Reusing an internal link does not widen its audience, and neither a portal session nor a customer-shareable purpose grants access to internal metadata. Clean evidence binds atomically with the case or incident action; a completed upload alone does not advance either record.

Ordinary employees can create general/typed-source reports through current `records.write` authority and read their own non-sales-order tickets through `records.read`; this does not grant broad `support.read`. Authorized Support roles retain scoped triage visibility. Typed POS sale, PMS record and asset links recheck owner permission, current source version and Company/Branch/Department scope at creation, replay and file access. Current source access is required even for a previously created ticket; revocation removes access.

Current employee/source lifecycles and independent resolution are mapped to integration and desktop/mobile cases in [the catalogue](catalogue.md#support). Historical role-denied browser observations do not establish current workflow coverage.
