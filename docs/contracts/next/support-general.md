# General and employee Support reports

The Support page accepts a direct issue report with a title and description. It does not require a sales order, customer, owner or follow-up date. The report enters `triage`; a Support manager assigns an eligible Support colleague and a follow-up date before work begins. The assigned colleague records progress and a proposed resolution. A different Support manager confirms a general issue's outcome with a reason or returns it for more work. Immutable events, optimistic versions, idempotency and current scope/recipient guards apply to each command. An unassigned report can be cancelled by a Support manager.

The existing sales-linked Support path remains separate. It still validates the quotation or sales order and current source version, snapshots the customer, and requires a recorded customer contact after resolution for independent closure. A general report cannot be confirmed with a customer contact note. General reports remain excluded from customer-order access sessions, shared customer incidents and field-service source selection. Their independently reviewed Support Portal binding below is a separate publication, not an order session.

Support files have two non-interchangeable audiences. `internal_evidence` follows the current assigned colleague/eligible Support-manager boundary. `customer_shareable` requires a Support manager and becomes visible only as a sanitized preview to the authenticated customer bound to the same approved order. Reusing an internal link does not widen its audience, and neither a portal session nor a customer-shareable purpose grants access to internal metadata. Clean evidence binds atomically with the case or incident action; a completed upload alone does not advance either record.

Ordinary employees can create general/typed-source reports through current `records.write` authority and read their own non-sales-order tickets through `records.read`; this does not grant broad `support.read`. Authorized Support roles retain scoped triage visibility. Typed POS sale, PMS record and asset links recheck owner permission, current source version and Company/Branch/Department scope at creation, replay and file access. Current source access is required even for a previously created ticket; revocation removes access.

Current employee/source lifecycles and independent resolution are mapped to integration and desktop/mobile cases in [the catalogue](catalogue.md#support). Historical role-denied browser observations do not establish current workflow coverage.

## Service parts preparation on the product host

The Service parts add-on has closed preparation pages at `services/field-service/settings/parts` and `services/field-service/settings/initial-stock[/countId]`. Company Finance prepares original charts/accounts/periods/rates and explicitly selects the original nine-role goods mapping; an existing valid mapping uses a distinct unchanged-map confirmation. Company/Branch Stock prepares original products/warehouses and an original independently reviewed physical Count. All operations intersect `service.orders`, `service.parts-assets`, `finance.service-invoice`, current native authority and the Field Service/Inventory/Finance releases. They add no Stock Core or Supply purchase, assignment or generic Finance/People access.

The Count is a first observation of an actually absent item/warehouse position, posted through the original current-period variance account and period. It is never opening equity or fabricated purchase history. Immutable native mapping/Count receipt relationships retain their original IDs, private Files/Flow/source authority and withdrawal obligations. Finance reference and currency-setting images are rechecked after deferred effects in the same transaction. Retained account identities remain readable after lawful later recoding; new posting still validates current semantics. The additive1062 schema is source only until its normal reviewed installation; absent schema produces an explicit readiness result. The development source and selected native cases do not establish installed migration or product acceptance.

Fresh Service mapping also binds the current independently approved Sales edition, even when no economic history exists. It preserves chart, document currency, receivable/revenue and effective VAT; independently approved null VAT remains null through the original effective reader. Finance leases and rereads every actual edition row after deferred effects. The optional identity is not default-inserted into old request bodies, preserving original receipt hashes. Existing-mapping confirmation remains a distinct unchanged-map operation; exact replay compares current before/after sources without requiring historical defaults to remain current.

## Ticket-bound Support Portal

The Helpdesk `support-portal` add-on has its own staff workflow at `customers-sales/customers/service/portal[/bindingId]` and verified-account public workflow at `/customer-portal/{holdingSlug}/tickets`. Current Support managers select an actual authorized ticket, explicitly prepare a customer label/email and minimal public subject/summary, then a different eligible manager approves the binding. It requires actual `support.tickets` and `support.portal`, current release and native authority even when generic legacy enforcement is disabled. The optional link on ordinary Helpdesk details hides expected unavailable/denied access without changing the strict named workspace. No Sales purchase, commerce transaction, customer-party creation or replacement access assignment is introduced. Field Service-managed tickets stay with their original owner.

Public readers use normal central Account/Portal verification and an independently approved unexpired binding pinned to the verified account. They see only its explicit publication, published responses and currently available `customer_shareable` sanitized previews. Original/private files, raw issue/events and source snapshots remain private; this constructor provides no public upload. Original Files availability, immutable scan/preview metadata and current revocation still govern preview bytes. Every response acknowledgement is an immutable receipt of one exact published response; it never resolves or closes the Support ticket. The original independent Support confirmation remains necessary.

Holding units retain source/purchase/native-authority leases, before/after owner images and read-only checks after deferred effects; current Control session checks remain held across private work. Every staff receipt must have its exact native binding event or response, including historical retries after lawful later decisions. Both base and add-on withdrawal obligations include retained draft/unexpired bindings; reject/revoke resolves access, while immutable history is retained. Source1065 is additive and remains uninstalled until the normal reviewed migration/restore and installed workflow acceptance. See [ADR048](../../adr/ADR-048-ticket-bound-support-portal.md).
