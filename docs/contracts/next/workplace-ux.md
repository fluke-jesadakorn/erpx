# Workplace navigation and work queues

Daily navigation is **Today, My work, Calendar, Messages and Start**. Phones expose exactly **Today, My work, Start, Messages and Apps**; Calendar is one link from Today and My work. Desktop keeps the 260px sidebar and the workspace-scoped 72px rail, single-open category accordion, keyboard flyouts and focus return on Escape.

The shared discovery order is People & organization → Customers & sales → Purchasing → Inventory & quality → Projects & routines → Services → Finance → POS → Hotel PMS → Production → Knowledge → Insights → Administration. Authorized ERPX Holding administrators also retain ERPX Management; Platform keeps its separate navigation and authority. A category contains direct work destinations, with detailed views in the owner's tabs. Empty categories are omitted. The catalogue gives all 33 modules a category, objective, canonical entry and AI support level; it does not activate a module. Current permissions and effective Company rollout determine displayed destinations.

Customers & sales has one Customers entry for relationship history, then Pipeline, Follow-ups, quotations and orders. Its customer directory remains a discoverable secondary destination. CRM accounts/contacts, Finance customers and PMS guests retain their different identities and reviewed links. Purchasing and Inventory retain separate ownership. People employment work and Administration access work share organization context while preserving their independent state and authority. Approval routing is an Administration setting, distinct from the Approvals decision queue. Guides and Company handbook are in Knowledge; courses and training results remain Learning records. Help using ERPX and Product roadmap are secondary destinations.

The App launcher, workspace search, Sidebar and shortcuts consume the same permission-filtered, URL-deduplicated navigation model. Search includes the six daily views and Start intents. Categories organize discovery and do not become domain parents or breadcrumb segments. Existing URLs, Company/workspace, hotel property, Finance chart and record filters remain valid. Shared commerce directories retain `?view=parties&kind=customer|supplier`; order details retain the actual `view=sales|quote|purchase`.

## Today, My work and shortcuts

My shortcuts shows at most four ordered entries in the full Sidebar. It reuses the existing versioned Work-card preferences, accepts stable destination IDs and recommends entries by role. Existing six-pin selections are preserved; All actions and Customize expose the remaining authorized destinations. Shortcuts on this surface do not repeat queue counts. Legacy navigation preference rows remain intact. Family tones identify People, commercial, service, supply, Finance and shared work, while status colors continue to carry separate outcome labels.

My work opens with `view=actions` and six principal views: My actions, Approvals, Tracking, Team, For me and Completed. Team is shown when relevant. Tracking offers All tracking, My requests and Waiting; it includes the user's initiated work and non-team items waiting on someone else. More retains Waiting, My requests, All work and authorized Routing attention for compatibility. Legacy `queue`, `responsibility`, `type` and `q` links remain valid. Type filters use authorized work present in the current snapshot, while preserving an explicitly selected legacy type. Return links retain these filters, Tracking and pagination.

Adapters supply actual `canAct`, decision/work/override kind, owner, next step, blocker and version. Approvals contains only an actionable approval or authorized override, rather than every item related to an approval. Preparation, receiving, posting and payment remain work actions. Setup without an owner is grouped in Team; eligible assigned IT work stays in My actions. For me links to Calendar and available personal shifts, leave, overtime, expense tracking, benefits and learning. Features without a released personal destination are omitted. Incomplete source or capability reads show their limitation and do not publish a complete action count.

Today uses human conversations as its main column. Latest updates, Following and accessible team/branch filters apply to conversations and recent system milestones. Team filters only narrow the existing membership and scope checks. The right rail contains compact Needs you, Start and My day. Phones place a brief Needs you/Calendar/Plan summary before the feed and open day tools on demand. `kind=system` entries appear in a collapsed Recent work milestones summary, grouped by linked source; their post history remains available. Work notifications for an active Quality hold share its work context and keep separate notification history. PMS links identify Reservation, Folio, Refund, Shift, Housekeeping task or the actual source type. A linked post's primary action is Open linked work; Create additional task remains an explicit secondary action. Photo-only entries retain previews or descriptive attachment fallbacks. My work counts actions; Today signals new team updates. Messages keeps Messages, Teams and Notifications with source links and independent unread state.

## Review, creation and cross-team work

The approval review panel opens from My work and returns to its current queue. Owner readers supply the submitter, facts, authorized evidence, recorded route, current/next step, blockers and permitted decisions for purchase/service/equipment requests, expenses, leave and tasks. Detail and route versions must agree. Accounting preparation, accounting reviews, posting, payment, leave cancellation and department overrides open the full owner workspace. Protected evidence is disclosed only through the owner's authorized reader.

Approve, Reject and supported Return decisions use the existing owner commands with the read version, a reason, explicit human confirmation and a stable idempotency key for the same retry. There is no generic approval write endpoint. Permission or version conflicts clear the loaded decision and require a fresh review. Owner commands still enforce self-review restrictions, current access and each workflow's state machine. Approval routing edits affect future submissions according to the existing saved-route rules.

Start presents Buy, Expense, Leave, OT, Task, Post, Handover, Get help and Plan my day, plus authorized Legal advice. AI intake prepares a proposal; direct owner forms remain available. Existing draft and evidence upload recovery retain the exact idempotent command and saved draft ID after a lost response. A browser reload cannot restore selected local files, so the user reselects any missing upload.

Cross-team work follows start → prepare → review/decide → execute/handoff → verify → complete through existing source workflows. Purchase request/RFQ/order, partial receiving, matching, posting and payment remain independent decisions. Marketing/CRM, quotation/order, fulfilment/invoice and collection retain customer identities and source context. HR employment, future-dated access and IT preparation remain independent states. Internal service requests, customer support, maintenance, field service and Legal retain their owning workflow and scope. Project context, routine templates and each generated task retain distinct histories. Social posts, reactions, notification reads and Daily plan never decide a business transaction.

Shared Source Record references carry kind, ID, permitted link, label and an available read version in Work, Social, Notifications and AI. Legacy data stays valid when a source lacks that version or adapter. A notification whose owner denies the current role is omitted without interrupting the surrounding Inbox page. Front POS/PMS offers Today, My work, Messages and Handover under More and preserves a canonical same-Holding/Company Front return with its location and record query. PMS Today uses `homeProperty`, `homeView` and `homeLabel` to restore its filters, leaving the existing `property` operational-dashboard link intact. The console shows Return to POS/Hotel PMS within the current work context. Employment, accounting, shift and hotel command authority remains with the respective owner.

## Ask ERPX

The shared assistant is named **Ask ERPX**, with Explain, Prepare, Check and Summarize. It labels the current support level: workflow guides; authorized records and summaries; or draft preparation and checks. Guides cover catalogue destinations. Read adapters support the existing owner records plus recent Today conversations/milestones and the current first page of My work, explicitly identifying partial data. Draft intake preserves the existing supported intents and takes users to the owner form for review. Catalogue-level guide support does not claim a new record reader or draft command.

AI receives only authorized source data and citations, retains available record versions and existing provider/context governance, and uses the owner to validate prices, leave eligibility, routing and computed results. It does not submit, approve, post, pay or change source authority. The manual guide/form remains usable when AI is unavailable. Production AI opt-in and module acceptance gates remain in force.

## Page layouts and My work navigation

Custom page layouts are available only for People directory and profile details, Organization & positions, Roster, People settings, Payroll and payroll-run details, Overtime and overtime-request details, Retirement clearance, and the Executive report library. Executive canvas arrangement and resizing remain available through their separate dashboard system. Handbook, Learn, Technology, Access, account and platform pages, and all other modules use fixed layouts.

Fixed pages use their declared widget order, original default widths and automatic heights. Saved personal and organization overrides on retired pages are no longer read or applied. These pages do not load the layout editor, request layout snapshots, offer layout controls, or register unsaved-layout warnings. Existing records and audit receipts remain stored; no schema migration or data deletion is required. The shared UI/API configurable-page allowlist rejects GET and POST for retired keys with `404 PAGE_LAYOUT_UNKNOWN`.

Retained layouts stay scoped to user, Company, workspace base and page key, with the existing permission-filtered widgets, personal overrides, organization publishing, reset, version checks and idempotency. Move, hide/show, width and height editing, cancellation and legacy widget focus remain available. Organization layout refers only to the Company widget baseline; Windows policies remain part of Technology.

My work has no in-page queue tabs or More views section. Its submenu contains My actions, Approvals, Tracking, Team, For me, Completed, Waiting, My requests, All work and Routing attention. Team is shown only when the team capability or a team relationship is present; Routing attention is shown only when routing work exists. The sidebar provides direct links, while mobile and collapsed-sidebar My work open a responsive view menu. Query-based direct links remain supported, and Tracking filters, search, responsibility, work-type filters and pagination retain their behavior.

Finance exposes To collect, To pay, To post and To reconcile queues linked to the existing invoice, ledger and bank screens. POS readiness leads to register/shift setup while cashier and kitchen stay separate operational views. PMS preserves the booking and reservation/stay/folio workflows and their original records.

## Account time and member profiles

An account starts with the browser/operating-system timezone and an English 24-hour clock. Its owner may choose a valid IANA timezone and a 12-hour clock from My profile; the preference is stored once in Control and follows the account across Holdings and devices. User-facing instants use the Gregorian calendar, English month names and the event's `UTC±HH:MM` offset. A date without a time remains a date. UTC storage and business rules remain unchanged. Generic date-time forms show the selected zone, reject nonexistent DST minutes and require an offset choice for repeated minutes. Roster, hotel, accounting and other policy wall clocks retain their explicit business timezone.

`/holdings/{holdingSlug}/profiles/{userId}` is a responsive member page with cover, avatar, work identity, introduction, up to three HTTPS links, About and Posts. The account name and work title come from their existing sources. A profile belongs to one Holding and opens only while both viewer and subject have active access in that Holding. Posts come from the current Company through the existing feed visibility predicate; cross-Company, branch, department and participant restrictions remain in force. Private HR contact, compensation and access details are absent. Owners may edit their introduction, links and images through versioned/idempotent commands. Image uploads accept JPEG, PNG and WebP (avatar 2 MB, cover 5 MB), require a clean scan and store only processed private WebP output. Image reads check current Holding access; removing an image clears its public profile reference and deletes the private object.
