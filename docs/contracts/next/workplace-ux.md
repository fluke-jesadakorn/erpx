# Workplace navigation and work queues

Daily navigation is Today, Calendar and Messages, with Start work and Apps actions. On desktop and tablet, the 260px business-workspace sidebar then renders permission-aware app links under People & organization, CRM, Sales, Procurement, Inventory, POS, Hotel PMS, Projects & Routines, Finance, Management and System in that order when available. The authorized ERPX Holding also shows ERPX Management in its existing position above Apps by work. Empty categories are omitted. CRM contains its customer relationship pages, Customer directory and Customer support. Sales contains orders, quotations and price lists; Procurement contains purchase orders, supplier quotation requests and Suppliers. Inventory contains stock work and Quality; Projects & Routines contains projects, staff tasks, routine templates and approval routing. Categories organize discovery only and do not become domain parents, routes or breadcrumb segments. Deep destinations remain in their owning app's local tabs and destination catalog. Platform navigation is separate and unchanged.

The shared commerce records keep their URLs. `?view=parties&kind=customer` and `kind=supplier` select separate, paginated customer and supplier directories; old party links resolve to the matching directory. Order detail links carry `view=sales|quote|purchase`, and old or mismatched detail links resolve the actual record kind before displaying navigation and breadcrumbs. Existing record commands, permissions, and saved widget layouts are unchanged.

The full sidebar is a single-open accordion. The current app's category opens automatically. It can collapse to a 72px icon rail, whose category buttons open keyboard-accessible flyouts; both the rail state and the last manually opened category are stored per workspace in local storage. Focus returns to a flyout trigger on Escape and interactive targets are at least 44px. Phones expose exactly five bottom controls: Today, Calendar, Start work, Messages and Apps.

Apps opens the App launcher. Its default view contains only app cards under the same permission-filtered categories. Searching expands into deep destinations and shows owning-app context such as `Finance › Bank reconciliation`; the launcher and `⌘K` consume the same URL-deduplicated destination catalog. Favorites, saved pins and the navigation-preference API are no longer active. Historical `navigation_preferences` migrations and rows remain intact for data preservation but are ignored by this navigation model.

## Work and Home

The authenticated holding layout presents one quick-access shelf in a non-sticky right rail beside page content on wide screens. Below 1200px, the same shelf moves below the workspace header as a collapsed disclosure, opening into a three-column tablet or two-column phone grid. It uses the existing company-wide Work card preferences and offers up to six ordered pins, including My shifts when roster access is available. Home's personal destinations appear in this catalog instead of a second Home-only strip. The navigation response supplies authorized cards and preferences without returning Work records; Work's queue still owns the full item list. On phones, the first four pins appear until Show all is selected. The shelf is outside the print view and follows the skip-to-content link in keyboard order.

Shortcut tiles and Work rows use the same fixed family tones: People violet, commercial blue, service teal, supply green, finance amber, and cross-cutting work brand blue. Every Work type has an explicit family mapping. Status badges retain their separate status colors, and color is never the only indicator of meaning.

Work opens with `view=actions`, with Waiting, My requests, Team and Completed tabs and All work under More. Legacy `queue`, `responsibility`, `type` and `q` URLs continue to work. Domain adapters supply `canAct`, operational/setup category and a setup reason; this presentation does not confer command authority. Due items sort oldest deadline first, then undated work. Setup without an owner appears grouped in Team. Explicitly assigned eligible IT work remains in My actions. Partial sources are labelled and never presented as a complete zero count.

Home places its first three authorized priorities above the team feed, using the same actionable work source and ordering. Each priority names its work family, next action, blocker when present, responsible person or queue, and real due date when present. It appears once on Home; the rail is for starting work and planning the day. Photo-only feed entries show a preview or a descriptive fallback with a link to the attachment. Work's badge counts My actions. Home remains a team news/conversation feed. Inbox separates Messages, Teams and Notifications; notifications resolve the authorized source record and retain its conversation, without a second approval queue.

Across light and dark themes, the visual hierarchy uses Geist with 34px page titles, 23px section titles, 17px card titles, 15px body text and 13px metadata. The ERPX blue marks primary actions and active navigation. Work-family colors identify categories; status colors identify outcomes, always with accompanying text. Shared surfaces use stronger text contrast, restrained corner radii and minimal decorative glow.

## Record and creation patterns

Record pages for requests, expenses, orders, invoices, journals, payroll, tasks, stock counts and transfers expose a shared status/responsible-person/next-action summary before detailed sections and history. The existing domain controls retain their independent approvals and state machines. Work return links preserve whitelisted filter parameters and workspace context.

`OperationPresentation` is display data derived from each authorized domain read. It carries status, responsible person, current step, next action, blocker, real due time, source-record link and action kind. It never grants a decision or changes a command route. Work preserves a separate item when the same record has both ordinary approval work and an authorized department override; the override is labelled as a decision on behalf of the assigned approver. The server decides who can see and perform either action. Calendar receives only authorized appointments and owned operational deadlines; approval creation or decision timestamps do not create Calendar events.

Approval history shows the route captured when the request was submitted, including ordered assignees, department flow version or appointed head, person or Position selector, current step, approver changes and override actor, original approver and reason. Publishing a new department flow affects new submissions only. Appointment and flow editors show their current version, effect, validation and save or conflict result. Company and Manage ERPX use the same work and record presentation through their domain adapters. Front POS/PMS retain a short Thai status, next action and primary action view.

New work groups personal requests and operational work by current creation capabilities, using intent labels and examples. Requests and expenses support evidence in the creation flow. A request stores the exact idempotent draft command before upload; network recovery reuses the command, locks the saved fields and retains failed uploads for retry. On reload, the draft ID is recovered and the user is told to reselect files the browser cannot restore. Existing edit forms, source permissions and optional AI review remain authoritative.

People, Approvals, POS and PMS share a readiness checklist with missing items, responsible roles and links to resolve them. Projects, Routines, Handbook, Learn, Billing and Administration reuse page headers, filters, empty/error states and the accessible Dialog. Preview capabilities retain their existing restrictions.

## People views and layouts

Standard People pages keep People directory, Roster, Organization & positions and authorized Payroll access in the feature tab bar. Company handbook, Learn and authorized People settings remain at their existing URLs and in global navigation, but appear under **People tools** inside the **People views and layout** drawer. Beta People retains its existing three-tab navigation. Both lists are derived from the same permission-filtered navigation model; moving a destination into the drawer never grants access.

The drawer also edits the current page's permission-filtered widgets. Layouts remain scoped to the user, Company, workspace base and page key. A personal layout is compared in the client with the reconciled **Organization layout** baseline for visibility, relative order, width and height. This comparison is presentation data and is not persisted. Reset widget restores that widget's organization visibility, geometry and ordinal position without discarding other personal changes. Reset all removes the personal override. Authorized organization managers may publish a new Organization layout; existing personal layouts remain unchanged until their owners reset them.

Layout changes preview on the page before Save and retain pointer/keyboard movement, resizing, hidden-widget restoration and legacy widget links. Cancel, drawer close and in-app navigation request confirmation when a draft is dirty. The modal drawer traps focus, restores the trigger, closes with Escape or backdrop, and prevents background scrolling. It is a fixed right drawer above 650px and a safe-area-aware bottom sheet at 650px and below.

**Organization layout** refers only to the Company-wide widget baseline. Windows Group Policy/GPO belongs to Technology → Groups & policies and is not part of page-layout comparison or publishing.

Finance exposes To collect, To pay, To post and To reconcile queues linked to the existing invoice, ledger and bank screens. POS readiness leads to register/shift setup while cashier and kitchen stay separate operational views. PMS preserves the booking and reservation/stay/folio workflows and their original records.

## Account time and member profiles

An account starts with the browser/operating-system timezone and an English 24-hour clock. Its owner may choose a valid IANA timezone and a 12-hour clock from My profile; the preference is stored once in Control and follows the account across Holdings and devices. User-facing instants use the Gregorian calendar, English month names and the event's `UTC±HH:MM` offset. A date without a time remains a date. UTC storage and business rules remain unchanged. Generic date-time forms show the selected zone, reject nonexistent DST minutes and require an offset choice for repeated minutes. Roster, hotel, accounting and other policy wall clocks retain their explicit business timezone.

`/holdings/{holdingSlug}/profiles/{userId}` is a responsive member page with cover, avatar, work identity, introduction, up to three HTTPS links, About and Posts. The account name and work title come from their existing sources. A profile belongs to one Holding and opens only while both viewer and subject have active access in that Holding. Posts come from the current Company through the existing feed visibility predicate; cross-Company, branch, department and participant restrictions remain in force. Private HR contact, compensation and access details are absent. Owners may edit their introduction, links and images through versioned/idempotent commands. Image uploads accept JPEG, PNG and WebP (avatar 2 MB, cover 5 MB), require a clean scan and store only processed private WebP output. Image reads check current Holding access; removing an image clears its public profile reference and deletes the private object.
