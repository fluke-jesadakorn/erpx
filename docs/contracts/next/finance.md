# Finance workspace contract

Finance is organized around five work areas: Today, Documents, Cash, Accounting and Reports. The Company ledger remains the accounting authority. Group reporting reads versioned Company balances and records separate consolidation snapshots; it never rewrites Company journals.

Finance and refund clients retain the submitted body, command key and original form edit revision after an uncertain response. Their command helpers offer caller acknowledgement: decoding a cloned response does not complete the attempt while the actual hook is still reading its original body. Only a typed receipt under the same live work context can acknowledge that exact response once. Revision changes, stale or removed work boundaries, unreadable receipts and unrelated later forms keep the attempt available for an exact retry. Newer edits retain their draft and do not receive the earlier command's local success. Server authority and transactional receipt checks remain mandatory; these client guards supply no permission.

## Fixed assets, issued statements and refund carrying evidence — 2026-10-03

`/finance/assets` and `/finance/assets/{id}` own unrecognized draft preparation, versioned editing and cancellation, independent acquisition recognition, monthly straight-line depreciation and independently posted disposal. Recognition debits the cost account and credits the reviewed acquisition offset. Recognition and the legacy monthly depreciation form select active Company dimensions; the API carries those values into every primary and secondary journal line, owner event and decision evidence. Missing or invalid required values abort the complete transaction. Old posted rows and command hashes are retained. Depreciation debits the configured expense and credits accumulated depreciation while retaining original cost; the final month allocates the residual cent exactly.
