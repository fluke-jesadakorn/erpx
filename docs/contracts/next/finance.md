# Finance workspace contract

Finance is organized around five work areas: Today, Documents, Cash, Accounting and Reports. The Company ledger remains the accounting authority. Group reporting reads versioned Company balances and records separate consolidation snapshots; it never rewrites Company journals.

## Current workspace

- `/finance` is Today. Source-owned work identities and actual next-action authority determine My actions, Waiting and Team/setup views. Cash signals remain separate counts; they are not added into a misleading total. Search and pagination are retained in the URL.
- `/finance/invoices?kind=receivable` and `?kind=payable` are the collection and payment obligation lists. Existing invoice and payment detail routes remain stable.
- A posted non-expense invoice can start a controlled receipt or payment. Company Finance prepares it, an independent Finance approver approves it, and a third Treasury user releases and confirms an exact imported bank row. Confirmation posts the balanced bank/control-account journal, allocates one or more compatible invoices, settles the payment, and reconciles the bank row atomically. Refunds continue through their return/refund obligation owner.
- A foreign-currency invoice records its transaction amount, recognition-date functional-currency rate and functional carrying amount. Bank confirmation records the settlement-date rate, preserves both sides of each allocation, and posts a separate realized gain/loss journal against configured functional-currency accounts. Foreign bank statements retain their transaction closing balance, closing rate and functional closing balance. At period end, Company Finance prepares closing rates and gain/loss mappings; an independent Finance approver revalidates the source hash and posts the unrealized revaluation. Open foreign items without a posted revaluation for that period block close.
- `/finance/accounting` owns manual journals and the journal list. `/finance/ledger` redirects here for compatibility.
- `/finance/close` shows period blockers plus account-level AR, AP, and bank-to-GL differences in functional currency. It also owns period-end foreign-currency revaluation preparation, independent approval, rejection and retained rate evidence. Close preparation stores an immutable versioned reconciliation snapshot; independent approval recomputes the same source evidence and rejects any stale or changed balance. Configured bank control accounts require a statement dated at period end; foreign statements use their retained closing rate.
- `/finance/reports` presents Company and chart context with the posted trial balance, category totals, independently issued NPAEs statement editions and reviewed fiscal closing.
- `/finance/group` reads intercompany transactions and immutable consolidation-run snapshots for the Holding.
- `/finance/setup` holds readiness checks and opening-balance import, separate from recurring accounting work.
- `/finance/engines` also contains recurring accrual schedules. Finance prepares a draft with two active same-currency accounts, an amount, date range and monthly/quarterly/annual frequency; an independent approver activates it. Each due period is prepared as a draft journal and must be posted by a different Finance approver. Posting advances the schedule cursor, marks the final period complete, and retains an immutable schedule/run event history.

All views use the selected Company from the active work context. Group Finance is visible only in a Holding workspace and still requires `finance.read`. Mutations continue to require the owner permission, idempotency key, current work authority, and optimistic version where applicable.

## Accounting and AI boundary

The existing expense AI can extract receipt evidence and prepare a proposal. A Finance Proposal is advisory: pinned source versions, evidence, provider revision, result, and human disposition are retained. AI never approves, posts, releases money, reconciles a bank transaction, closes a period, or publishes a consolidation run. Those actions stay on deterministic services with independent human authority.

The workspace assistant may explain only authorized data supplied to it. Report totals are calculated by finance queries rather than by a language model. If AI is unavailable, document preparation and every control action remain usable.

Both expense workflow versions share Finance chrome for authorized Finance viewers; a cancelled claim with missing amounts displays Not provided instead of inventing a zero or failing to render. Printed record views use readable light document colors, span the full page and exclude internal work assignment and command controls.

Creation has one entry point per page. Forms appear only in the create view or an explicit disclosure; balances, workflow help, calculations and retained history use progressive disclosure. ## Company Finance AI policy and proposals

Migrations `0919_finance_ai_proposals.sql` and `0920_finance_ai_runtime_grants.sql` add Company policy, source-versioned proposals, request reservations and retained events. The policy defaults off. An authorized Company assignment with `finance.read` and `ai.manage` explicitly chooses capabilities, timezone, daily/monthly limits and a reason. Its captured assignment/role revision delegates advisory preparation; changing the active console role does not revoke this explicit policy, while revoked or changed access does. No assignment or additional financial authority is created. Receipt extraction also requires an available image provider and the existing expense stage owner/evidence controls.

`GET /finance/ai` reads the current policy, provider availability and retained proposals. `POST /finance/ai/policy`, `/finance/ai/prepare`, `/finance/ai/{id}/dismiss` and `/finance/ai/{id}/apply-draft` use current work authority, idempotency, Company scope and optimistic versions. Enabled kinds cover daily briefs, document coding/posting review, collection text, payment preparation, exact compatible bank matches, tax/close evidence and report explanations. Original receipt/page OCR is reused instead of creating a second file pipeline. No external message or financial action is sent by the scanner.

The scheduled PostgreSQL worker scans explicitly enabled Company policies, reserves at most two preparations per scan and drains bounded jobs. Automatic document/movement preparation deduplicates by source version, daily briefs and report snapshots by business date; an explicit fresh request can include changed evidence. Leases renew during generation and compare ownership before publication. The worker rechecks assignment, production module, policy version, provider revision and source hash before and after AI. Company financial owner receipts conservatively invalidate aggregate proposals after any relevant financial source change. A stale proposal retains evidence and audit history and cannot save a draft.

Generated JSON must cite supplied source IDs/direct fields, use available distinct chart/currency accounts and select bank payments only from deterministic same-bank/currency/signed-amount candidates. Taxed document suggestions require a separate tax account. AI summaries are advisory and require human evidence review; they do not establish statutory eligibility or entitlement. Request reservations, including failures and explicit retries, share the Company AI allowance across entry points under a PostgreSQL advisory transaction lock.

The workspace presents the 30 most recent proposals, initially six with Show more; immutable source facts, retained results and decision history remain available per proposal. This bounded recent list is not a complete audit search or a statutory register.

The one direct draft action applies reviewed coding to an existing standalone draft invoice through the original `finance.invoice.update` command. Proposal disposition, selected-account evidence, original invoice validation, flow responsibility, version checks, receipt and audit commit together; a rejected owner command rolls everything back. People can correct suggested accounts, must provide a reason, and still need independent posting approval. Other proposals supply copyable text or links to their normal owner workflow. Manual work remains available when the policy is off, access changes, the provider fails or a quota is exhausted.

Before a production release, apply both migrations with the authorized environment's migration role before starting the updated jobs runtime. Development migration uses only the existing `erpx_dev_*` manifest. No production promotion, staging access, WHT/50-tawi completion, statutory statement certification or filing certification is implied by this redesign.

## Production boundary

The current implementation provides the operating workspace over the existing Company accounting, payment, reconciliation, and close services. Advanced baseline tables for tax, assets, budgets, leases, loans, revenue schedules, intercompany transactions, and consolidation runs remain disabled for production use until their lifecycle controls, calculations, filing/export validation, and acceptance fixtures are completed. Presence of a table or setup check is not production certification.

Opening a real ledger requires accountable review of the Company chart, periods, tax policy, bank accounts, approval assignments, source-module mappings, and opening trial balance. A pilot must complete two consecutive closes with subledger-to-GL and bank reconciliations before go-live.

## Thai accounting development foundation — 2026-09-30

The [active production plan](../../production-plan.md) retains the complete four-phase delivery scope. [Official research](../../testing/thai-accounting-research-2026-09-30.md) and [implementation evidence](../../testing/evidence/2026-09-30-thai-accounting-foundation.md) distinguish source verification, developer fixtures and business acceptance. This foundation does not promote a production gate.

`/finance/setup` can create the first THB chart and period, import accounts and opening balances, and prepare an Accounting Profile. A different Finance approver reviews NPAEs eligibility evidence, the statutory chart, fiscal start month, effective date and Company-confirmed policies. THB, current tax, moving average and straight line are supported product policy choices; profile approval is not a central posting-rule or VAT-registration decision. Approved profiles are retained unchanged. An effective approved profile supplies the default report book; without one, more than one Company chart, including archived charts, requires explicit selection. Account imports cannot change referenced names, categories, currencies or control meanings.

AR/AP aging accepts `asOf` and `chartId`. Dated recognition, allocation, return, refund, reversal and FX evidence reconstruct balances rather than reading today's outstanding amount. A correction in a later open period leaves the earlier cutoff intact. The close calculation uses signed refund balances so a paid return awaiting refund reconciles with its control account. Legacy documents or allocations without dated journal links are explicitly labelled as limited evidence and block close even when their total matches GL; migration must supply reviewed opening-item evidence. Issued statement packages retain a separate versioned reporting snapshot as described below.

Trial balance and category statements accept `chartId`, `periodId`, `startDate` and `endDate`. Profit and loss uses the selected interval; the balance sheet carries posted balances through the end date and includes cumulative unclosed profit in equity. The issued NPAEs workflow adds reviewed account presentation, equity movements, all five note groups, comparative dates and immutable editions to these ledger readers.

`POST /finance/journals/batch-post` accepts exact draft versions and a review reason. Each batch is limited to 100 manual/opening-import drafts and 1,000 lines in one Company, chart, open period and transaction currency. Each draft requires an independent assigned reviewer, unchanged line evidence and active valid accounts. All postings, immutable Financial Events, individual review evidence, audit and command receipts commit together; concurrent retries replay one result. Module-owned drafts reject the batch until their source/rule adapter is implemented. Individual module posting continues through its owner lifecycle.

Close preparation checks both draft and in-review documents/journals, subledger/GL evidence, bank statements, returns, unresolved money instructions and effective statutory VAT review. VAT-to-GL differences also block preparation; VAT evidence joins the close hash and retained check snapshot and is rechecked inside approval's transaction. `POST /finance/periods/{id}/cancel-close` requires the current close version and reason, and the preparer or Finance approver; it returns a prepared closing period to open while retaining previous reconciliation/check versions. Approval of an old preparation fails after cancellation or evidence changes.

### Source-derived VAT and filing preparation

`/finance/tax` and `/finance/tax/packages/{id}` provide the initial THB register and ภ.พ.30 preparation flow. The originating posted invoice or completed Commerce return, its immutable Financial Event and posted tax-account lines determine taxable and VAT amounts. The preparer supplies document reference, tax-point evidence, legal basis, claim month and deduction treatment; an independent Finance approver checks source and party versions. Document date, accounting date, VAT point, input-claim month and external filing date stay separate. Supported treatments are standard, zero, exempt, prohibited input and proportional input; returns refer to the approved original entry and retain its deduction policy.

The interfaces are `GET /finance/tax-register?chartId&filingMonth`, `POST /finance/tax-entries`, `/finance/tax-entries/{id}/approve|reject`, `POST /finance/vat-packages`, `/finance/vat-packages/{id}/approve|export|file|supersede`, and `GET /finance/vat-packages/{id}` or `/{id}/export`. Commands retain current authority, Company scope, optimistic versions and idempotency. Package preparation freezes the reviewed register and VAT-account reconciliation; source and GL snapshots are asserted in the transaction and checked again on approval/export. Superseding an unfiled package retains its contents. Additional preparation references a filed original and retains both results. Human filing-reference/receipt evidence is recorded separately from exporting; downloading a retained export returns its original bytes and hash.

Approved VAT entries can now be corrected through `POST /finance/tax-entries/{id}/corrections` and independent `POST /finance/vat-corrections/{id}/approve|reject`; `GET /finance/tax-entries/{id}` and `/finance/vat-corrections/{id}` supply history and before/after evidence. English pages at `/finance/tax/entries/{id}` and `/finance/tax/corrections/{id}` expose the workflow. Only the latest approved entry may be replaced. An invoice correction includes every reviewed linked Commerce return, with signed deductions and references to the new original. The original approved rows remain unchanged; the current register contains approved leaves only and hides correction drafts until joint approval. One pending correction is allowed per invoice family, at most 100 replacement entries per correction and 100 pending correction groups per Company. Every member, decision, audit and replay receipt commits together; deferred database constraints reject partial family decisions and changes to frozen draft evidence.

Family/source/party versions and independent current authority are rechecked. Changed source families require rejecting and preparing a fresh correction. Pending corrections block package/close preparation. Approved corrections stale unfiled packages and prepared close support, even when only a document reference changes and GL totals remain equal. Superseded, exported and filed snapshots retain their original entry versions and export hashes; package pages explain current-register changes. Filing receipts remain recordable against an already exported version using actual evidence, even after a correction. Tax-point, claim-month, document evidence and deduction corrections do not alter posted taxable/VAT amounts or create accounting journals. Any required accounting reclassification needs a separately authorized adjustment; this is not automatic correction of the originating business transaction.

The UTF8 BOM/CRLF CSV is an **ERPX preparation workpaper**, not an RD import file or an officially validated return. Spreadsheet-executable text is escaped. The package excludes prior credits and does not determine final additional-return deltas, penalties, tax-payment liabilities or settlement. The register supports one whole-document VAT point for THB invoices/returns, not partial services, advances, foreign tax bases or split VAT points. Central effective-dated legal rules, Company/branch VAT registration, issued Thai documents and sequence controls, WHT/50 ทวิ and RD V2.0/SWC validation remain required. Generic calculation-engine review cannot approve the source-VAT package's calculation through a second workflow.

## Fixed assets, issued statements and refund carrying evidence — 2026-10-03

`/finance/assets` and `/finance/assets/{id}` own unrecognized draft preparation, versioned editing and cancellation, independent acquisition recognition, monthly straight-line depreciation and independently posted disposal. Recognition debits the cost account and credits the reviewed acquisition offset. Depreciation debits the configured expense and credits accumulated depreciation while retaining original cost; the final month allocates the residual cent exactly. Disposal reverses cost and accumulated depreciation, records proceeds and the resulting gain or loss, and retires the asset atomically. Every source, monthly posting and decision remains linked in immutable asset history. A positive-cost operational asset requires a one-to-one recognized Finance link; its physical retirement must be pending and open Maintenance work completed before Finance disposal can post.

`POST /finance/assets`, `/assets/{id}/edit|cancel|activate|depreciate|disposals` and `/asset-disposals/{id}/post|cancel` require current Company Finance authority, source versions, an idempotency key and an open compatible period for posting. Acquisition and disposal require a different approver. Cost currencies and shared dated money evidence must be ready before new writes; an absent manual quote fails explicitly.

`POST /finance/statement-packages` freezes the approved NPAEs Accounting Profile revision, source ledger revision, account presentation, current and preceding comparative dates, financial position, income, equity movements and the five required note groups. `/statement-packages/{id}/issue|reject|cancel` independently reviews the current draft. Changes to source accounts, journals or Company policy stale an unissued preparation. An issued package has retained HTML bytes and SHA256, available at `/statement-packages/{id}/export`; an amended edition references the earlier issued package and preserves its source, notes and export. `/finance/statement-packages/{id}` displays the complete snapshot and decision history. The retained export is an internal statement package with engineering evidence, and does not imply a DBD filing receipt or accountant certification.

`POST /finance/year-closings` prepares one complete approved fiscal year against the actual P&L accounts and retained earnings mapping. Earlier accounting periods and the preceding fiscal result must be closed. `/year-closings/{id}/post|reject|cancel` rechecks the source revision and independently posts a balanced fiscal-transfer journal. The original income statement excludes this closing transfer; source-issued statements retain the same reported result after closing. Reopening a period preserves the posted fiscal transfer and issued copies; manual commands cannot forge the owner source or post backdated P&L changes into a transferred year.

Foreign Commerce credits retain their original posted control carrying amount. An unpaid share reduces the current source invoice carrying value, any reviewed difference posts a separate realized-FX journal, and the paid share becomes an immutable refund obligation with source credit journal and base amount. Finance instructions freeze transaction and carrying amounts before review/export. Partial bank confirmation allocates original carrying value cumulatively, consumes exact independently imported bank evidence and posts cash plus any realized-FX difference atomically. Historical foreign refund rows without reliable carrying evidence remain explicitly unready for a new payment rather than receiving an invented historical rate.

Payable invoice posting also consumes clean File Sources sets under `supplier_bill / bill_match_support / action / invoice-review` in the same independently authorized posting transaction. The original document and tax-document record panels retain current owner version and Company scope. Replayed commands return their retained result only after current authorization.

## Native itemized and ordinary Thai tax documents

Standalone invoice commands accept either the complete native `lines` revision or the existing whole-document header inputs. Native lines retain exact three-decimal quantity, integer two-decimal unit price, account/VAT/source-version snapshots and complete immutable revision history. Header totals must equal the line sums; posting uses independently reviewed source accounts and versions. Source-owned Commerce and matched supplier bills retain their original owner interfaces. New Finance money writes require a dated shared quote and explicitly supported two-decimal currency scale; JPY/KWD are rejected rather than reinterpreted. Historical amounts keep their recorded meaning.

`/finance/thai-tax` prepares legal identities through `/finance/tax-identities` and independent `/{id}/decide`. A Company payer draft binds its real scanned signature under `finance_tax_identity / signer_signature / record`; approved identities and signature hashes are retained. Ordinary domestic supplier payment classification uses `/finance/payments/{id}/withholding` and `/finance/withholding/{id}/decide`. Current invoice income, VAT, contract totals, recipient type, effective reviewed identity dates and the pinned [official ordinary Thai rule pack](../../testing/thai-finance-tax-rules-2026-10-03.md) determine the exact independently reviewed tax. Configured Companies cannot settle an unclassified ordinary supplier payment through a different batch owner. Preparer, reviewer and Treasury remain distinct.

Actual bank confirmation allocates gross AP while posting net bank and withheld liability. It immediately retains two recipient 50 ทวิ copies and one payer copy, sequential legal number, actual source versions, bound signature and raw HTML SHA256. `/finance/thai-documents/{id}` and its `/export` preserve issued bytes; `/changes` followed by independent `/finance/thai-document-changes/{id}/decide` produces a separate identity amendment. Financial amount corrections use the original payment reversal, which retains cancelled document events and source-linked negative gross/net/tax money.

`POST /finance/withholding-months` captures actual posted payment dates and retained certificate hashes into an immutable PND3/PND53 manual workpaper. Independent `/{id}/decide` review precedes a third Treasury person's `/{id}/remit`, requiring the exact current imported THB tax debit and manual filing receipt. The original export at `/{id}/export` retains its bytes/SHA. A supplemental edition includes previous sources and receipts but pays only new late tax. `/finance/withholding-remittances/{id}/reverse` requires an actual positive bank credit and independent recovery evidence before its source payment can be cancelled. Shared serializable transactions, optimistic guards and immutable assessment receipts prevent bank reuse and duplicate tax remittance. The monthly CSV is a manual review workpaper; it does not submit a registered filing or assert provider acceptance.

## Calculation engines

`/finance/engines` prepares deterministic, source-hashed calculation snapshots for exact allocation, indirect cash flow, Thai VAT/withholding, NCI/equity-method/goodwill, moving-average manufacturing cost, and many-to-many reconciliation controls. Money enters and leaves an engine as decimal strings with an explicit currency scale. Runs require an independent review and approval; they do not silently post a Journal, file a return, reconcile a bank movement, or publish group statements.

The schema retains effective-dated calculation policies, statutory packages, group entities and ownership, acquisitions, bank match groups, close checks, manufacturing cost movements and overhead pools. Each downstream posting/export/publish adapter must revalidate the approved run's source hash and business-specific controls before changing its authority record.

Recurring accrual commands are `POST /finance/accrual-schedules`, `/finance/accrual-schedules/{id}/approve`, `/finance/accrual-schedules/{id}/cancel`, `/finance/accrual-schedules/{id}/prepare`, and `/finance/accrual-runs/{id}/post`. They require current Finance authority and an idempotency key; an open accounting period must cover the due period before preparation, and a posted schedule run is a normal balanced journal linked to its source schedule.
