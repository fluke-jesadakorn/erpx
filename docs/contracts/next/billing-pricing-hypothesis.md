# Sandbox pricing hypothesis and feasibility gate

The original **read-only commercial experiment** is `sandbox-hypothesis-2026-09-v1`, with a THB 1,490 Holding fee and AI cost plus 20%. It remains historical preview data, separate from invoices. The intermediate fixed-fee target version is also provisional and cannot become a billable entitlement. [ADR-037](../../adr/ADR-037-sandbox-ai-gateway-and-rated-holding-billing.md) now defines the seat-only target Sandbox offering with AI cost plus **10%**. Existing customers retain [ADR-028](../../adr/ADR-028-sandbox-cost-plus-support-billing.md) Support billing until an explicitly assigned future month; no prior invoice is rerated. The new offering may issue only for designated Sandbox test accounts after completeness and approval gates pass.

## Proposed Holding model

One Holding receives one monthly bill for its Companies and Branches. The current target Sandbox offering is **THB 499 per active named seat-month from the first seat, with no fixed Holding fee**, with 2 **decimal** GB of original files included per full seat-month (10 seats = 20 GB), THB 5 per excess GB-month of byte-time, and statement-reconciled ERPX-funded AI provider expense plus 10%. A person with access to several Companies or Branches is one Holding seat. Seat time and included storage are prorated together; enabled access, not login activity or HR headcount, defines a seat. Customer-funded BYOK, failed requests, and simulation are excluded. Setup, data migration, and extra Support require separate approved proposals. Company and Branch breakdowns explain consumption but do not create additional seat duplicates.

The price catalog has an effective month and identifier. A later change appends a revision; frozen periods retain the earlier revision. Named-seat access time and original-file byte-time are clipped to the active service period. One active person counts once at any instant across the Holding; service and Platform identities are excluded. A current active-seat snapshot is displayed for visibility, but cannot stand in for the historical seat-time of an earlier or incomplete month. Historical access that cannot be proven holds the entire invoice. Token counts alone cannot produce an AI price: the funding source, provider units, per-request verified cost, and reconciled statement are required. Unknown funding, missing provider expense, or incomplete storage history displays **"Measured usage — price unavailable"** rather than THB 0. BYOK displays **"Paid directly to provider — not billed by ERPX"**. Shadow totals say **"Sandbox estimate — not an invoice"**.

## Current feasibility evidence

- The current default beta policy is 1 GiB per Company ([packages/server-manage_erpx/src/public/beta-config.ts](../../../packages/server-manage_erpx/src/public/beta-config.ts)), whereas the proposed allowance scales at 2 decimal GB per paid seat-month across the Holding. Raising the allowance requires a capacity decision and an enforcement change; the preview price is not an entitlement.
- `df -h .` on this workspace showed about 19 GiB available on 2026-09-27. Ten full-month seats would receive 20 decimal GB (about 18.6 GiB) before PostgreSQL growth, backups, derivatives, and other customers. The current disk cannot support that allowance safely.
- Gateway metering and cost-evidence intake have been added, but two full reconciled monthly provider statements, backup-amplified storage cost, infrastructure allocation, and Support time per Holding have not yet been accepted. A measured quantity with missing evidence stays unpriced.
- The public [Stripe Thailand Payments pricing](https://stripe.com/th/pricing) lists 3.65% + THB 10 for domestic cards and 1.65% for PromptPay; [Stripe Invoicing Starter](https://stripe.com/th/invoicing/pricing) lists 0.4% per paid invoice (checked 2026-09-27). A one-seat THB 499 bill would leave approximately THB 469 by domestic card or THB 489 by PromptPay at those published rates, before hosting, storage, AI, support, taxes, refunds, or account-specific pricing. These are feasibility examples, not net-revenue forecasts.
- THB 499 per seat-month with no fixed Holding fee is a Sandbox pilot hypothesis chosen after reviewing public [Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/th_TH/pricing) and [Zoho One](https://www.zoho.com/th/one/pricing/) per-user prices (checked 2026-09-28). Those products are not directly comparable, and their price is not evidence that ERPX's cost or margin is viable.

## Gate before any price approval

Collect at least two complete shadow billing cycles of actual cost per Holding: host and database allocation, original and derived files plus backup amplification, AI provider statements matched to usage, Stripe charges, and Support time. Reconcile the usage breakdown to the Holding total and account for shared or unassigned Company usage. Model gross margin under the real number of Holdings, then approve a price, storage entitlement, and operational capacity together. [ADR-037](../../adr/ADR-037-sandbox-ai-gateway-and-rated-holding-billing.md) authorizes designated Sandbox test invoices only; migration of existing customers and Live collection remain separate decisions.
