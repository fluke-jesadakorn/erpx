# AI and Collaboration Requirements

Collaboration and Agent Orchestration are shared bounded contexts. IAM owns
principals, Platform Administration owns providers, source contexts own records
and commands, and the Item Directory remains a disposable read projection.

| REQ-COL-016 | Recipient snapshot | ERPX shall pin group version and authorized recipient identities at send time. | Historical recipients must not drift. | Success: snapshot receives notifications; Denial: future members receive none; Failure: partial fan-out reconciles to the snapshot. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-55 |
| REQ-COL-017 | Broadcast capability | ERPX shall require explicit capability for company-, branch-, or holding-wide mention fan-out. | Organizational broadcast has outsized impact. | Success: authorized broadcast records audience and count; Denial: ordinary participant is rejected; Failure: limit breach prevents partial send. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-55 |
| REQ-COL-018 | Hierarchical discovery | ERPX shall apply holding-descendant, published-parent, and explicit sibling-sharing rules to hierarchical collaboration. | Hierarchy alone is not permission. | Success: allowed cross-scope mention resolves; Denial: sibling without agreement is hidden; Failure: stale hierarchy fails closed. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-48, E2E-49 |
| REQ-COL-019 | Follow lifecycle | ERPX shall auto-follow authors, assignees, and mentioned recipients and support authorized unfollow, mute, and snooze. | Participants need continuity and control. | Success: follower receives permitted updates; Denial: mandatory policy cannot be hidden; Failure: expired snooze restores delivery. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-45 |
| REQ-COL-020 | Record anchor | ERPX shall anchor a thread to a record, exact revision, field, block, page, or region with a digest. | Review comments must retain meaning. | Success: anchor reopens original target; Denial: inaccessible target is redacted; Failure: changed source marks stale/orphaned. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-54 |
| REQ-COL-021 | Encrypted draft | ERPX shall autosave online comment drafts encrypted in the anchor plane under per-user access and TTL. | Drafts need recovery without breaking isolation. | Success: user resumes on another device; Denial: another user cannot read it; Failure: revoked access deletes or seals the draft. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-57 |
| REQ-COL-022 | Realtime thread | ERPX shall stream authorized comment, revision, reaction, typing, and presence events. | People and agents need timely collaboration. | Success: subscribers converge in order; Denial: revoked subscriber stops receiving bodies; Failure: reconnect resumes from durable cursor. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-57 |
| REQ-COL-023 | Single-author editing | ERPX shall use optimistic concurrency for a comment draft or revision and shall not provide multi-author CRDT editing in the baseline. | Comment authorship must remain clear. | Success: current author saves exact version; Denial: concurrent stale save conflicts; Failure: local unsent state is not claimed as accepted. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-57 |
| REQ-COL-024 | External thread | ERPX shall expose only explicitly shared portal threads to external principals. | External collaboration needs a narrow boundary. | Success: external participant reads permitted blocks; Denial: internal notes/directory remain absent; Failure: expired portal authority revokes access immediately. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-58 |
| REQ-COL-025 | External mention alias | ERPX shall limit external mentions to approved aliases, teams, or external-safe agents. | External users must not enumerate internal identities. | Success: approved alias routes safely; Denial: raw workforce search is unavailable; Failure: unavailable alias creates no partial fan-out. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-58 |
| REQ-COL-026 | Comment retention | ERPX shall apply the strictest applicable anchor, comment-class, jurisdiction, customer, and legal-hold policy. | Collaboration cannot weaken source retention. | Success: eligible purge produces evidence; Denial: active hold blocks purge; Failure: unresolved policy fails closed. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-53 |
| REQ-COL-027 | Search and retrieval | ERPX shall index comments and make them available to AI only under exact current audience and provider policy. | Discovery must preserve comment restrictions. | Success: authorized result resolves source; Denial: hidden snippets/counts are absent; Failure: tombstone/deletion propagates before reuse. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-23, E2E-61 |
| REQ-COL-028 | Governed moderation | ERPX shall support report, quarantine, hide, restore, and policy redaction while preserving protected evidence. | Harmful content needs controlled handling. | Success: moderator action is attributable; Denial: ordinary owner cannot access sealed original; Failure: scanner uncertainty quarantines safely. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-67 |
| REQ-COL-029 | Typed action card | ERPX shall require a typed owner-context action card for any business mutation proposed in a thread. | Natural language has no business authority. | Success: authorized confirmation invokes source command; Denial: comment text/reaction cannot mutate; Failure: rejected command returns a correlated outcome. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-59 |
| REQ-COL-030 | Promote to work | ERPX shall promote an authorized comment revision to a task, issue, decision, or change request with bidirectional provenance. | Discussion should become accountable work without rewriting history. | Success: source owner creates one item; Denial: unauthorized type/scope is rejected; Failure: retry resolves the prior promotion receipt. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-59 |
| REQ-COL-031 | Tenant boundary | ERPX shall reject cross-tenant mentions and references except through the existing customer-approved platform-support boundary. | Collaboration must not create a new tenant bridge. | Success: approved support purpose resolves only allowed metadata; Denial: ordinary cross-tenant target is absent; Failure: expired or uncertain approval fails closed. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md), [ADR-006](../../adr/ADR-006-support-capabilities.md) | E2E-48, E2E-51 |

| REQ-COL-033 | Independent authorization stages | ERPX shall authorize directory discovery, autocomplete rendering, notification delivery, and record/thread opening as separate decisions using current policy. | Passing one collaboration stage must not grant the next. | Success: every permitted stage records its own decision; Denial: a previously notified but revoked principal cannot open content; Failure: unavailable policy suppresses that stage without reusing an older grant. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-45, E2E-48, E2E-51 |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| REQ-AGN-003 | Agent lifecycle | ERPX shall govern draft, approved, suspended, and retired agent states. | Only reviewed agents may run. | Success: approved version is discoverable; Denial: draft/suspended/retired agent cannot receive new work; Failure: running work follows revocation policy. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-46 |
