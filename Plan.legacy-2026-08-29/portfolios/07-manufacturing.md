# Manufacturing Requirements

Manufacturing owns production definitions, orders, execution, genealogy, WIP,
and production-cost facts. Inventory, Quality, Maintenance, Planning, and
Finance retain their respective authority.

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-MFG-001 | Manufacturing mode | ERPX shall classify each production definition as discrete or process and prevent silent conversion between modes. | The models have different quantity and genealogy semantics. | Success: mode pins valid controls; Denial: incompatible fields are rejected; Failure: conversion requires a new approved version. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-002 | Item production policy | ERPX shall define authorized make, buy, subcontract, and co-production policies by item, site, and effective period. | Planning and execution need one production intent. | Success: effective policy resolves deterministically; Denial: overlapping active policy is rejected; Failure: missing policy blocks release. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-34 |
| REQ-MFG-003 | Versioned BOM | ERPX shall govern bill-of-material versions, effectivity, alternates, and approvals. | Consumption must be reproducible. | Success: released order pins one approved BOM; Denial: draft/expired version cannot release; Failure: changed BOM does not alter existing order silently. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32 |
| REQ-MFG-004 | Versioned routing | ERPX shall govern routing operations, sequence, work centers, setup, run, and queue standards. | Capacity and cost depend on the approved route. | Success: order pins a valid route; Denial: missing work-center authority blocks release; Failure: later route changes preserve old execution evidence. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32 |
| REQ-MFG-005 | Formula and recipe | ERPX shall govern process formulas, scaling basis, yield, density, and effective version. | Batch production requires fixed-precision composition. | Success: batch quantities scale deterministically; Denial: incompatible units are rejected; Failure: precision overflow blocks release without rounding drift. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-006 | Co-products and by-products | ERPX shall distinguish planned co-products, by-products, waste, and rework outputs. | Output valuation and genealogy differ. | Success: each output has quantity and disposition; Denial: negative unexplained output is rejected; Failure: unmatched output creates an exception. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-007 | Production-order lifecycle | ERPX shall control create, approve, release, start, pause, resume, complete, cancel, close, and archive transitions. | Shop-floor state must be explicit. | Success: valid transition emits a receipt; Denial: invalid sequence is rejected; Failure: partial execution remains reconcilable. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-008 | Material reservation | ERPX shall request Inventory reservations without owning stock balances. | Production must not double-allocate materials. | Success: accepted reservation is source-linked; Denial: unavailable stock prevents unapproved release; Failure: timeout reconciles by idempotency key. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32 |
| REQ-MFG-009 | Material issue | ERPX shall accept authoritative Inventory issue facts against a released operation or batch. | WIP needs actual consumption. | Success: issue updates WIP once; Denial: wrong site/lot/order is rejected; Failure: duplicate facts are ignored and auditable. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-010 | Backflush policy | ERPX shall execute backflush only from a versioned policy and confirmed production quantity. | Automatic consumption must remain explainable. | Success: calculated issue reconciles to policy; Denial: missing or stale policy blocks backflush; Failure: variance creates correction work, not silent adjustment. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32 |
| REQ-MFG-011 | Labor and machine capture | ERPX shall record attributable labor and machine time by operation. | Actual cost and capacity need evidence. | Success: bounded time posts once; Denial: unauthorized worker/work-center is rejected; Failure: overlapping or invalid intervals require correction. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32 |
| REQ-MFG-012 | Operation completion | ERPX shall complete an operation only after required quantities, time, evidence, and quality gates are satisfied. | Downstream work depends on valid completion. | Success: completed operation unlocks successors; Denial: missing gate blocks completion; Failure: reversal uses an explicit correction. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32 |
| REQ-MFG-013 | Batch execution | ERPX shall record batch start, additions, transformations, samples, yields, and close against the pinned formula. | Process history must be reconstructable. | Success: batch ledger balances inputs and outputs; Denial: unapproved addition is rejected; Failure: unexplained variance prevents close. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-014 | Lot and serial genealogy | ERPX shall maintain bidirectional input-to-output lot and serial genealogy. | Recall and traceability require complete lineage. | Success: authorized trace reaches all linked lots; Denial: hidden scope nodes do not leak; Failure: lineage gap blocks release and raises an exception. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-015 | Quality release | ERPX shall require accepted Quality disposition before controlled output becomes available. | Manufacturing cannot self-approve regulated quality. | Success: accepted disposition releases exact quantity/lot; Denial: pending/rejected output remains blocked; Failure: stale disposition version is ignored. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-016 | Nonconformance handoff | ERPX shall hand production nonconformance to Quality with immutable source evidence. | Quality owns disposition. | Success: one case links order/operation/lot; Denial: Manufacturing cannot overwrite disposition; Failure: duplicate handoff reconciles to one case. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-017 | Maintenance availability | ERPX shall respect accepted Maintenance downtime and asset availability for work centers. | Production must not schedule unavailable equipment. | Success: unavailable capacity blocks execution; Denial: operator cannot ignore a safety lock; Failure: stale availability is explicit and fail-safe. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32 |
| REQ-MFG-018 | WIP ledger | ERPX shall maintain fixed-precision WIP quantities and values by order, operation, item, and cost element. | WIP must reconcile to sources. | Success: issues, labor, output, scrap, and close balance; Denial: direct unreferenced WIP edits are rejected; Failure: imbalance prevents close. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-019 | Production costing | ERPX shall publish accepted material, labor, overhead, scrap, and variance facts to Finance. | Finance owns accounting recognition. | Success: balanced source facts post once; Denial: Manufacturing cannot post journals directly; Failure: rejected handoff remains reconcilable. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-020 | Scrap and rework | ERPX shall record reasoned scrap and governed rework lineage. | Loss and recovery must be visible. | Success: approved disposition updates quantities; Denial: hidden scrap is rejected; Failure: rework loop limits prevent endless cycles. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-021 | Subcontract operation | ERPX shall coordinate subcontract operations through Procurement and accepted receipt facts. | External processing crosses ownership. | Success: service order and material custody reconcile; Denial: Manufacturing cannot fabricate vendor receipt; Failure: short/failed service creates an exception. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32 |
| REQ-MFG-022 | Production correction | ERPX shall correct posted execution through reasoned reversal or adjustment commands. | Auditability forbids history rewriting. | Success: correction references original fact; Denial: closed/held period rejects unauthorized change; Failure: partial reversal rolls back atomically. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-023 | Production close | ERPX shall close an order only when material, output, quality, WIP, and cost reconciliations pass. | Close certifies completeness. | Success: zero unresolved variances closes once; Denial: open issues block close; Failure: lost receipt is safely retrievable. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
| REQ-MFG-024 | Manufacturing evidence | ERPX shall expose authorized genealogy, execution, quality, and cost evidence with source freshness. | Operations and audit need one explainable record. | Success: evidence links every accepted fact; Denial: restricted formulas/costs are redacted; Failure: missing projection is explicit and rebuildable. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-32, E2E-33 |
