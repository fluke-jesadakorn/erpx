# Learning Records Requirements

Learning owns training assignments, sessions, attendance, and completion
records. The baseline is not a commercial LMS or a full assessment and
certification platform.

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-LRN-001 | Training catalog | ERPX shall version internal training definitions, providers, delivery mode, audience, and effective period. | Assignments need a stable definition. | Success: active definition is assignable; Denial: draft/expired definition is rejected; Failure: changed content creates a new version. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-38 |
| REQ-LRN-002 | Assignment policy | ERPX shall assign training by authorized person, role, organization, event, or governed rule. | Required learning must be attributable. | Success: eligible learner receives one assignment; Denial: out-of-scope learner is excluded; Failure: directory uncertainty prevents unsafe expansion. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-38 |
| REQ-LRN-003 | Assignment lifecycle | ERPX shall govern assigned, acknowledged, scheduled, in-progress, completed, waived, overdue, cancelled, and archived states. | Compliance records need explicit outcomes. | Success: valid transition records evidence; Denial: learner cannot self-waive; Failure: duplicate completion is idempotent. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-38 |
| REQ-LRN-004 | Session scheduling | ERPX shall govern session capacity, location, instructor, roster, waitlist, cancellation, and notices. | Delivery logistics need consistent control. | Success: confirmed roster respects capacity; Denial: unauthorized overbooking is rejected; Failure: cancellation notifies affected learners. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-38 |
| REQ-LRN-005 | Attendance | ERPX shall record attributable attendance, absence, lateness, and corrections. | Completion depends on reliable participation facts. | Success: instructor-authorized attendance posts once; Denial: learner cannot alter instructor evidence; Failure: correction references original record. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-38 |
| REQ-LRN-006 | Completion | ERPX shall record completion only when the governed attendance or provider criteria are satisfied. | Completion must be defensible. | Success: accepted evidence completes exact assignment; Denial: insufficient evidence is rejected; Failure: provider ambiguity remains pending. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-38 |
| REQ-LRN-007 | Provider result | ERPX shall reconcile external training results through a versioned provider adapter. | Providers are evidence sources, not ERPX authority. | Success: verified result advances once; Denial: invalid callback is rejected; Failure: out-of-order results reconcile. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-38 |
| REQ-LRN-008 | Waiver | ERPX shall require authorized reason, evidence, scope, and expiry for a training waiver. | Waivers cannot be informal comments. | Success: approved waiver satisfies only named assignment; Denial: self-waiver is rejected; Failure: expired waiver reopens obligation. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-38 |
| REQ-LRN-009 | People handoff | ERPX shall publish accepted training completion to People without editing employment state. | People owns workforce records. | Success: one completion fact links learner and definition; Denial: Learning cannot change role; Failure: rejected link becomes reconciliation work. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-38 |
| REQ-LRN-010 | Training history | ERPX shall provide an authorized learner and manager history with source and freshness. | People need evidence without excess exposure. | Success: permitted history is complete; Denial: unrelated managers cannot discover records; Failure: unavailable provider facts are labeled. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-38 |
| REQ-LRN-011 | Retention | ERPX shall apply workforce, jurisdiction, legal-hold, and course-evidence retention policies. | Training records may outlive a session. | Success: eligible records purge with proof; Denial: hold blocks deletion; Failure: unresolved policy fails closed. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-38, E2E-42 |
| REQ-LRN-012 | Scope boundary | ERPX shall not claim full assessment, credential issuance, proctoring, or commercial LMS capability without a later accepted decision. | Baseline scope must be truthful. | Success: supported records are clearly named; Denial: unsupported certification action is unavailable; Failure: provider data cannot imply an ERPX credential. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-38 |
