# Projects Requirements

Projects owns project definitions, work graphs, time/cost collection, and
project handoffs. Linked domain records remain under their owner contexts.

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-PRJ-001 | Project classifications | ERPX shall classify projects as client services, internal, or capital and pin the governing policy. | Accounting and billing differ by purpose. | Success: valid class activates matching controls; Denial: incompatible policy is rejected; Failure: reclassification requires governed transition. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35, E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-002 | Project lifecycle | ERPX shall govern propose, approve, activate, pause, resume, complete, close, cancel, and archive. | Project authority must be explicit. | Success: valid transition records a receipt; Denial: invalid sequence is rejected; Failure: partial close remains reversible before finalization. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35 |
| REQ-PRJ-003 | Hierarchical work graph | ERPX shall model Project, Phase or Epic, Item, and Sub-item as an acyclic governed hierarchy. | Large programs need structured decomposition. | Success: valid parentage resolves breadcrumbs; Denial: cycles/cross-tenant parents are rejected; Failure: concurrent moves use version checks. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-004 | Work item types | ERPX shall provide task, milestone, deliverable, risk, issue, decision, and change-request item types. | Different work needs distinct evidence. | Success: each type applies its approved template; Denial: unsupported type is rejected; Failure: template withdrawal blocks new use without corrupting old items. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-005 | Governed templates | ERPX shall version item statuses, transitions, fields, approvals, and mappings to core work states. | Customization must preserve common reporting. | Success: project pins one template version; Denial: arbitrary logic/code is rejected; Failure: migration requires preview and rollback evidence. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-006 | Project item route | ERPX shall give every project item an opaque identity, canonical `/project/item/:id` route, and stable `/reference/item/:id` resolver. | Items require durable links. | Success: authorized deep link resolves after route changes; Denial: hidden item is indistinguishable from absence; Failure: stale descriptor degrades safely. | [FSD-006](../decisions/FSD-006-clean-rebuild-architecture-and-cutover.md) | E2E-50, E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-007 | Ownership and assignment | ERPX shall distinguish accountable owner, assignees, contributors, watchers, and approvers. | Mentions must not change accountability. | Success: typed assignment changes roles; Denial: comment mention leaves ownership unchanged; Failure: orphaned work enters reassignment flow. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-26, E2E-45, E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-008 | Schedule | ERPX shall govern planned/actual dates, calendars, constraints, and milestone commitments. | Delivery forecasts need stable semantics. | Success: dates calculate in scoped calendars; Denial: invalid sequence is rejected; Failure: missing calendar marks schedule unknown. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35, E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-009 | Dependencies | ERPX shall record typed finish/start dependencies without permitting cycles. | Work order and impact must be explainable. | Success: valid dependency affects schedule; Denial: cycle is rejected; Failure: deleted predecessor produces governed orphan handling. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-010 | Cross-project agreement | ERPX shall require owners on both sides to accept, reject, revoke, or cancel a dependency that affects another project or branch. | One project cannot impose work unilaterally. | Success: accepted relation records both authorities; Denial: rejected or one-sided dependency remains non-blocking; Failure: revoke/cancel reconciles both graphs. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-65 |
| REQ-PRJ-011 | Baseline | ERPX shall version approved scope, schedule, cost, and resource baselines. | Variance requires an accepted reference. | Success: reporting compares to pinned baseline; Denial: unauthorized rebasing is rejected; Failure: changed scope requires a new approval. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35 |
| REQ-PRJ-012 | Time capture | ERPX shall record attributable time against eligible project items and periods. | Services and capital work require labor evidence. | Success: accepted time links worker/item/date; Denial: closed period or ineligible worker is rejected; Failure: corrections reverse original facts. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35 |
| REQ-PRJ-013 | Cost capture | ERPX shall accept source-linked expense, procurement, inventory, labor, and allocation facts. | Project cost must reconcile to source domains. | Success: facts post once with currency; Denial: direct unreferenced cost edits are rejected; Failure: source reversal updates variance. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35 |
| REQ-PRJ-014 | Budget control | ERPX shall compare commitments and actuals to versioned project budgets. | Owners need early control. | Success: threshold action follows policy; Denial: hidden costs are not exposed to unauthorized viewers; Failure: unavailable Finance facts show incomplete status. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35 |
| REQ-PRJ-015 | Resource plan | ERPX shall plan named or role-based resources without overriding People authority. | Capacity needs privacy-safe planning. | Success: eligible availability is reserved; Denial: restricted personnel data is hidden; Failure: assignment conflict becomes an exception. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35 |
| REQ-PRJ-016 | Client billing facts | ERPX shall publish approved billable time, expense, milestone, and fee facts to Sales or Finance. | Projects cannot own invoices. | Success: accepted fact creates one billing candidate; Denial: project user cannot post invoice; Failure: rejected candidate returns a correction reason. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35, E2E-66 |
| REQ-PRJ-017 | Capitalization handoff | ERPX shall hand approved eligible capital costs to Assets and Finance. | Capital projects cross accounting ownership. | Success: accepted costs create source-linked asset facts; Denial: ineligible/closed-period cost is rejected; Failure: partial handoff remains reconcilable. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35, E2E-66 |
| REQ-PRJ-018 | Domain action handoff | ERPX shall propose changes to linked Finance, Legal, Operations, or other domain records through approved typed owner-context requests with idempotency, correlation, and reconciliation responses. | References do not grant mutation authority. | Success: owner context executes one approved request and returns a receipt; Denial: Projects cannot edit invoice/contract/operation directly; Failure: rejection or uncertain outcome is returned and reconciled by correlation. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-66 |
| REQ-PRJ-019 | Risks and issues | ERPX shall distinguish uncertain risks from realized issues and preserve response ownership. | Governance needs different lifecycles. | Success: risk realization links resulting issue; Denial: closed risk cannot be silently reopened; Failure: missing owner blocks acceptance. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-020 | Decisions | ERPX shall record decision options, authority, outcome, rationale, and effective scope. | Project choices need durable evidence. | Success: accepted decision pins evidence; Denial: comment text alone is not a decision; Failure: supersession preserves the original. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-59, E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-021 | Change control | ERPX shall assess and approve scope, schedule, cost, and dependency impact before applying a change request. | Changes must not rewrite baselines silently. | Success: approved change creates a new version; Denial: maker-only approval is rejected; Failure: partial application rolls back. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-022 | Project close | ERPX shall close only after work, time, cost, billing, capitalization, dependencies, and evidence reconcile. | Close certifies completeness. | Success: all gates pass and close once; Denial: unresolved item blocks close; Failure: lost receipt is retrievable idempotently. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35, E2E-66 |
| REQ-PRJ-023 | Project reporting | ERPX shall report scope, schedule, cost, resource, risk, issue, and dependency state with provenance. | Stakeholders need explainable status. | Success: measures link sources and cutoff; Denial: hidden records/counts are excluded; Failure: stale contribution is labeled. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-35, E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-024 | Project archive | ERPX shall archive project records under retention, legal hold, reference, and customer-exit policy. | Completed work remains governed. | Success: authorized archive is read-only and exportable; Denial: active hold blocks deletion; Failure: unresolved references prevent destructive purge. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-31, E2E-35 |
| REQ-PRJ-025 | Core work-state mapping | ERPX shall map every versioned project workflow to `proposed`, `open`, `in-progress`, `waiting`, `resolved`, `closed`, or `cancelled` core states. | Enterprise work and reporting need stable cross-template semantics. | Success: each custom state maps to exactly one core state; Denial: an unmapped or contradictory template cannot activate; Failure: withdrawn mapping leaves pinned items readable and blocks unsafe transition. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-64 |
| REQ-PRJ-026 | Complete project item | ERPX shall give every first-class project item an owner, assignees, dates, status, dependencies, comments, attachments, references, time/cost facts, and canonical route under applicable field authorization. | Large changes need one accountable work record without centralizing linked domain authority. | Success: authorized detail resolves all present facts with source/freshness; Denial: hidden fields and linked items are redacted without counts; Failure: unavailable source is labeled and cannot be silently assumed. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-64, E2E-66 |
