# Recruiting and Talent Requirements

Recruiting & Talent owns requisitions, candidates, selection, offers,
performance, succession, and compensation proposals. Accepted hires and
compensation outcomes hand off to People & Payroll. Internal and candidate
experiences are responsive Web-only.

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-TAL-001 | Separate candidate realm | ERPX shall keep candidate identity separate from workforce and customer realms until accepted linking. | Application does not grant employment access. | Success: candidate accesses only own applications; Denial: matching email does not merge credentials; Failure: link conflict requires verified resolution. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-002 | Requisition lifecycle | ERPX shall govern requisition draft, approval, publication, hold, closure, cancellation, and archive. | Hiring demand needs authority and budget. | Success: approved requisition publishes; Denial: unapproved/closed requisition rejects applications; Failure: changed requisition invalidates pending publication. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-003 | Position and budget handoff | ERPX shall validate requisition position and budget through Organization, People, and Finance facts. | Talent does not own organization or budget. | Success: accepted facts pin scope; Denial: unavailable authority blocks approval; Failure: changed position returns requisition to review. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-004 | Job publication | ERPX shall publish only approved, effective, privacy-reviewed job content. | Candidate-facing content is governed. | Success: pinned version appears on allowed channels; Denial: internal/restricted fields are excluded; Failure: partial publication reconciles. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-005 | Application lifecycle | ERPX shall receive, withdraw, retain, erase, and archive applications under candidate and jurisdiction policy. | Candidate data needs explicit lifecycle. | Success: candidate receives a durable receipt; Denial: another candidate cannot inspect it; Failure: duplicate submission is idempotent. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-006 | Candidate profile | ERPX shall version candidate-submitted facts, attachments, consent, and provenance. | Selection must use known evidence. | Success: review pins a profile version; Denial: restricted data is hidden; Failure: malware or invalid attachment is quarantined. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-007 | Selection workflow | ERPX shall govern stage transitions, evaluators, scorecards, conflicts, and reasons. | Hiring decisions need consistent evidence. | Success: authorized evaluator records one result; Denial: conflicted/self-approval is rejected; Failure: incomplete required scorecard blocks advancement. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-008 | Interview scheduling | ERPX shall coordinate interview slots, participants, candidate notices, and changes without exposing internal calendars. | Scheduling crosses realms. | Success: confirmed slot notifies bounded participants; Denial: candidate cannot browse staff calendars; Failure: conflict returns alternatives. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-009 | Offer lifecycle | ERPX shall govern offer creation, approvals, exact-version presentation, acceptance, rejection, expiry, and withdrawal. | Employment terms require precise authority. | Success: candidate acts on pinned offer; Denial: changed/expired offer cannot be accepted; Failure: callback retry preserves one outcome. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-010 | Hire handoff | ERPX shall hand an accepted candidate and approved terms to People & Payroll exactly once. | People owns employment. | Success: one employee-onboarding request retains provenance; Denial: Talent cannot activate employment; Failure: rejected handoff returns correctable reasons. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-011 | Candidate communications | ERPX shall separate transactional recruiting messages from marketing consent. | Applying is not marketing permission. | Success: required status messages deliver; Denial: marketing suppression remains effective; Failure: adapter outage retains inbox evidence. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-012 | Performance cycles | ERPX shall version cycle scope, goals, reviewers, forms, calibration, acknowledgement, and closure. | Performance evidence must be comparable and bounded. | Success: eligible participants complete pinned forms; Denial: unauthorized peer/manager access is rejected; Failure: changed reviewer triggers reassignment. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-013 | Goal evidence | ERPX shall preserve goal versions, progress sources, assessment, and final outcome. | Retrospective edits must not rewrite evaluation. | Success: outcome links exact goal state; Denial: closed-cycle edits are rejected; Failure: source unavailability is disclosed. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-014 | Calibration | ERPX shall enforce authorized calibration groups, conflicts, changes, and final approval. | Cross-team comparison is sensitive. | Success: approved calibration records rationale; Denial: hidden employees/counts are not exposed; Failure: incomplete group blocks finalization. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-015 | Succession planning | ERPX shall govern critical-role, candidate, readiness, risk, and review facts under restricted access. | Succession data is highly sensitive. | Success: authorized plan shows scoped candidates; Denial: ordinary managers cannot discover hidden plans; Failure: stale employment status flags review. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-016 | Compensation proposal | ERPX shall govern merit, bonus, promotion, and adjustment proposals without posting payroll. | Talent can propose but Payroll owns pay. | Success: approved proposal hands off exact amounts/effective dates; Denial: proposer cannot self-finalize when SoD applies; Failure: Payroll rejection returns reason. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
| REQ-TAL-017 | Talent retention | ERPX shall apply candidate, employee, jurisdiction, legal-hold, and erasure policies separately. | Candidate and employee records have different purposes. | Success: eligible data is deleted with evidence; Denial: active hold blocks purge; Failure: unresolved identity link fails closed. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-37, E2E-42 |
| REQ-TAL-018 | Talent evidence | ERPX shall expose authorized requisition-to-hire and talent-decision provenance without leaking restricted comparisons. | Audit and disputes need traceability. | Success: evidence links approvals and versions; Denial: protected ratings/compensation are redacted; Failure: missing evidence blocks sign-off. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-37 |
