# Supply and Demand Planning Requirements

Planning owns plans and recommendations, not source demand, inventory,
procurement, manufacturing, or execution authority.

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-PLN-001 | Planning policy | ERPX shall version planning horizons, calendars, time fences, lot rules, and release authority. | Runs must be reproducible. | Success: run pins one policy; Denial: overlapping active policy is rejected; Failure: missing policy blocks execution. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-002 | Demand intake | ERPX shall ingest versioned independent and dependent demand without mutating its source. | Planning needs authoritative demand. | Success: accepted demand is source-linked once; Denial: unscoped demand is rejected; Failure: missing versions are quarantined. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-003 | Supply intake | ERPX shall ingest on-hand, reservations, open supply, and capacity with source freshness. | Recommendations depend on current supply. | Success: snapshot declares positions and cutoffs; Denial: hidden branches are excluded; Failure: stale sources are visible and policy-controlled. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-004 | Scenario isolation | ERPX shall keep draft scenarios isolated from released business state. | Planners need safe alternatives. | Success: scenarios clone pinned inputs; Denial: draft output cannot create supply; Failure: incomplete clone is discarded. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-005 | Deterministic MRP | ERPX shall calculate time-phased net requirements deterministically from pinned inputs. | The same plan must be repeatable. | Success: replay produces the same outputs; Denial: floating-point values are rejected; Failure: arithmetic overflow fails without partial plan. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-006 | BOM explosion | ERPX shall explode only effective approved production structures. | Proposed materials must be valid. | Success: dependent demand links the structure version; Denial: draft BOM is excluded; Failure: cycle detection blocks the affected plan. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-007 | DRP | ERPX shall calculate inter-site replenishment recommendations from scoped demand, supply, lead time, and transfer rules. | Distribution must balance sites. | Success: recommendation names source/destination; Denial: unauthorized cross-branch plan is rejected; Failure: missing lane becomes an exception. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-008 | Capacity requirements | ERPX shall calculate load by work center, calendar, and time bucket. | Material feasibility alone is insufficient. | Success: overloads identify contributing orders; Denial: hidden cost/person data is excluded; Failure: unavailable calendars mark capacity unknown. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-009 | Constraint exceptions | ERPX shall surface shortages, lateness, overload, policy conflicts, and stale inputs as typed exceptions. | Planners need actionable explanations. | Success: exception links cause and recommendation; Denial: inaccessible causes are redacted; Failure: unresolved exception prevents release when policy requires. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-010 | Pegging | ERPX shall maintain authorized demand-to-supply pegging for every recommendation. | Users must understand allocation impact. | Success: trace identifies quantities and dates; Denial: hidden demand is not disclosed; Failure: broken peg invalidates release. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-011 | Planner overrides | ERPX shall record reasoned overrides without rewriting calculated baseline output. | Human judgment must be attributable. | Success: override shows before/after and actor; Denial: unauthorized override is rejected; Failure: stale scenario requires rebase. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-012 | Scenario comparison | ERPX shall compare scenarios using pinned measures and assumptions. | Decisions need like-for-like evidence. | Success: comparison declares input differences; Denial: inaccessible scenario is absent; Failure: incompatible versions block comparison. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-013 | Human release | ERPX shall require an authorized human release before recommendations become source-context requests. | Planning has no autonomous business authority. | Success: approved selection creates typed requests; Denial: AI or draft run cannot release; Failure: changed recommendation invalidates approval. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-014 | Procurement handoff | ERPX shall hand approved buy recommendations to Procurement idempotently. | Procurement owns purchase orders. | Success: one request maps to one source decision; Denial: Planning cannot create a PO directly; Failure: rejection returns a reason for replanning. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-015 | Manufacturing handoff | ERPX shall hand approved make recommendations to Manufacturing idempotently. | Manufacturing owns production orders. | Success: accepted request preserves plan linkage; Denial: Planning cannot release production directly; Failure: changed feasibility returns an exception. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-016 | Transfer handoff | ERPX shall hand approved transfer recommendations to the owning inventory/operations workflow. | Transfers cross site authority. | Success: both scopes and lane are validated; Denial: sibling scope without agreement is rejected; Failure: partial acceptance remains reconcilable. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-017 | Plan reconciliation | ERPX shall reconcile released recommendations with accepted, changed, rejected, completed, or cancelled source outcomes. | Plans must learn actual disposition without owning it. | Success: status updates once per source fact; Denial: planner cannot rewrite source outcome; Failure: gaps appear as reconciliation work. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
| REQ-PLN-018 | Planning evidence | ERPX shall retain scenario inputs, policy, calculations, overrides, release, and handoff evidence. | Planning decisions must be explainable. | Success: authorized replay reconstructs output; Denial: restricted demand is redacted; Failure: missing evidence prevents certification. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-34 |
