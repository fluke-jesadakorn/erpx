# Benefits Requirements

Benefits owns plan configuration, eligibility, enrollment, elections, and
provider reconciliation. Payroll owns deductions and providers own claims.

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-BEN-001 | Benefit plan | ERPX shall version plan, option, coverage, contribution, provider, effective, and jurisdiction facts. | Enrollment must pin exact terms. | Success: active version is selectable; Denial: draft/expired plan is rejected; Failure: overlapping versions require resolution. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-002 | Eligibility rules | ERPX shall evaluate eligibility from approved employment, organization, date, and policy facts. | Benefits cannot redefine employment. | Success: decision records inputs and policy; Denial: ineligible election is rejected; Failure: missing source fails closed. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-003 | Enrollment window | ERPX shall govern open enrollment and eligible life-event windows. | Elections require a valid period. | Success: eligible employee opens exact window; Denial: late election is blocked without exception authority; Failure: timezone ambiguity is resolved by policy. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-004 | Election lifecycle | ERPX shall govern draft, submitted, approved, rejected, active, changed, waived, terminated, and archived elections. | Coverage state must be explicit. | Success: valid transition records receipt; Denial: employee cannot activate election directly; Failure: concurrent submission is version-checked. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-005 | Dependent identity | ERPX shall maintain scoped dependent identity and relationship evidence without creating workforce authority. | Dependents require privacy protection. | Success: authorized employee manages eligible dependents; Denial: unrelated users cannot discover them; Failure: duplicate identity requires governed resolution. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-006 | Coverage evidence | ERPX shall require configured evidence for elections and life events. | Providers and policy may require proof. | Success: valid evidence satisfies named rule; Denial: malware/unauthorized document is quarantined; Failure: missing evidence keeps election pending. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-007 | Contribution calculation | ERPX shall calculate employee/employer contributions with fixed precision from pinned policy. | Payroll deductions must reconcile. | Success: replay returns identical amounts; Denial: floating-point input is rejected; Failure: unresolved currency/rate blocks approval. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-008 | Approval | ERPX shall apply maker-checker and exception approval to governed elections. | Sensitive exceptions need independence. | Success: eligible approver decides exact version; Denial: maker/self-approver is rejected; Failure: changed election invalidates approval. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-009 | Payroll handoff | ERPX shall hand approved effective deduction facts to People & Payroll exactly once. | Payroll owns payslip deductions. | Success: accepted handoff links election/version; Denial: Benefits cannot edit payroll run; Failure: rejection returns a correctable reason. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-010 | Payroll reconciliation | ERPX shall reconcile deduction acceptance, change, rejection, reversal, and termination. | Enrollment and payroll must agree. | Success: status converges to Payroll facts; Denial: Benefits cannot mark deduction posted; Failure: mismatch creates governed work. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-011 | Provider enrollment | ERPX shall exchange approved enrollment through a versioned provider adapter. | Provider delivery is external. | Success: accepted file/API result links elections; Denial: unapproved provider receives nothing; Failure: partial rejection is itemized. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-012 | Provider reconciliation | ERPX shall reconcile provider acknowledgements and eligibility differences without overwriting ERPX approvals. | Systems may disagree. | Success: each difference has disposition; Denial: callback cannot silently alter election; Failure: unresolved mismatch remains visible. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-013 | Life-event change | ERPX shall apply effective-dated life-event changes with evidence and retroactivity policy. | Coverage changes require timing control. | Success: approved change produces future/retro facts; Denial: unsupported retroactivity is rejected; Failure: payroll/provider conflicts require reconciliation. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-014 | Termination | ERPX shall terminate coverage from accepted employment or eligibility facts and publish downstream changes. | Departures must stop benefits accurately. | Success: effective termination reconciles providers/payroll; Denial: user cannot erase history; Failure: provider delay is explicit. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-015 | Claims boundary | ERPX shall not adjudicate insurance or medical claims in the baseline. | Provider claims are outside ERPX authority. | Success: approved integrations expose bounded status only; Denial: claims decision commands are unavailable; Failure: imported status cannot imply ERPX adjudication. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
| REQ-BEN-016 | Benefits evidence | ERPX shall retain authorized plan, eligibility, election, approval, payroll, and provider evidence under privacy policy. | Employee disputes require provenance. | Success: evidence reconstructs coverage decision; Denial: health/dependent data is restricted; Failure: missing evidence blocks verified outcome. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-39 |
