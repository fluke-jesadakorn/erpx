# E-commerce Requirements

E-commerce owns channels, listings, carts, and checkout orchestration. Customer,
Sales, POS, Inventory, Finance, and fulfillment contexts retain their records.
The workspace and external journeys are responsive Web-only.

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-ECM-001 | Channel lifecycle | ERPX shall govern commerce-channel draft, approval, activation, suspension, retirement, locale, currency, and scope. | External surfaces require controlled publication. | Success: active channel resolves approved settings; Denial: draft/suspended channel is unavailable; Failure: invalid configuration prevents activation. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-002 | ERPX-controlled edge | ERPX shall expose commerce only through ERPX-controlled Web BFFs and provider adapters. | No public developer platform is authorized. | Success: approved edge validates every call; Denial: customer API keys are unavailable; Failure: adapter outage degrades without bypass. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-003 | Listing projection | ERPX shall compose sellable listings from approved product, brand, price, tax, and availability facts. | Commerce must not own source catalogs. | Success: listing identifies source versions; Denial: unentitled/restricted item is absent; Failure: stale critical facts suppress purchase. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-004 | Publication workflow | ERPX shall approve and schedule listing publication and withdrawal. | Public changes need evidence. | Success: pinned version publishes at effective time; Denial: maker cannot self-approve when policy forbids; Failure: partial channel publish rolls back or reconciles. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-005 | Price display | ERPX shall display price, currency, tax basis, and effective conditions from accepted commercial facts. | Buyers need truthful totals. | Success: displayed price matches checkout source; Denial: unauthorized price list is hidden; Failure: unresolved price prevents checkout. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-006 | Availability display | ERPX shall present bounded availability without treating projections as stock reservation. | Browse and fulfillment authority differ. | Success: freshness is visible where material; Denial: hidden-location quantities are not exposed; Failure: stale supply is shown conservatively. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-007 | Cart lifecycle | ERPX shall create, update, expire, merge, and abandon carts under channel and identity policy. | Carts need deterministic ownership. | Success: authorized changes update one cart version; Denial: another customer cannot read it; Failure: concurrent edits return conflict. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-008 | Guest identity | ERPX shall isolate guest commerce identity from workforce, candidate, and customer credentials until explicit linking. | Email equality is not identity proof. | Success: verified link preserves consent and cart; Denial: matching email grants no account access; Failure: link conflict requires resolution. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-009 | Checkout snapshot | ERPX shall pin cart lines, price, discounts, tax, shipping, customer, address, and terms for checkout. | Acceptance must reference an exact offer. | Success: order request matches snapshot; Denial: changed terms invalidate checkout; Failure: partial snapshot is discarded. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-010 | Inventory reservation | ERPX shall request and reconcile Inventory reservation before order acceptance when policy requires. | Overselling must be controlled by Inventory. | Success: accepted reservation binds exact lines; Denial: E-commerce cannot create stock; Failure: timeout resolves through idempotent status query. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-011 | Payment intent | ERPX shall request provider-authoritative payment intent through Finance/POS policy without storing payment instruments. | Commerce must minimize payment data. | Success: approved intent returns bounded status; Denial: unapproved provider is rejected; Failure: uncertain payment reconciles before retry. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-012 | Order handoff | ERPX shall hand an accepted checkout to Sales or POS exactly once. | Source order ownership must be unambiguous. | Success: one checkout maps to one order receipt; Denial: E-commerce cannot mutate accepted order; Failure: retry returns prior handoff. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-013 | Fulfillment visibility | ERPX shall present accepted fulfillment and shipment facts without editing them. | Customers need status from source authority. | Success: timeline names freshness; Denial: unrelated customer is denied; Failure: missing provider events show unavailable state. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-014 | Cancellation request | ERPX shall route cancellation requests to the current order/fulfillment owner. | Cancellation eligibility changes over time. | Success: owner returns accepted/rejected result; Denial: Commerce cannot force cancellation; Failure: concurrent shipment produces a reconciled outcome. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-015 | Return request | ERPX shall collect return reason and evidence and hand the request to Sales, POS, Inventory, Quality, or Support policy. | Returns cross domains. | Success: one request obtains authoritative disposition; Denial: invalid window/item is rejected; Failure: partial handoff remains visible and retryable. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-016 | Refund visibility | ERPX shall display accepted refund status from the payment/Finance authority. | A return is not a refund. | Success: status and amount reconcile; Denial: E-commerce cannot mark paid/refunded; Failure: uncertain provider state is explicit. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-017 | Commerce consent | ERPX shall separate transactional necessity, marketing consent, suppression, and preference authority. | Checkout cannot imply marketing permission. | Success: required notices and optional consent are distinct; Denial: suppressed marketing stays blocked; Failure: consent unavailability fails closed for marketing only. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-05, E2E-36 |
| REQ-ECM-018 | Commerce evidence | ERPX shall retain channel, listing, checkout, handoff, payment, fulfillment, return, and consent provenance. | Disputes require an exact journey. | Success: authorized evidence resolves all source receipts; Denial: payment secrets are excluded; Failure: gaps prevent a complete-evidence claim. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-36 |
