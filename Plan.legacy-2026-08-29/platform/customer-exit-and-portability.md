# Customer Exit and Portability Requirements

Customer exit is a governed lifecycle spanning platform control, customer and
customer-hosted data planes, providers, identity, retention, and evidence.

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-PRT-001 | Exit request | ERPX shall create a scoped, approved, versioned customer-exit case with accountable authorities. | Exit is destructive and cross-system. | Success: valid request pins tenant/holdings/mode; Denial: unauthorized request is rejected; Failure: changed scope invalidates approval. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-31 |
| REQ-PRT-002 | Export inventory | ERPX shall inventory authoritative records, projections, objects, comments, agent sessions, providers, identities, routes, holds, and evidence. | Completeness must be known before exit. | Success: inventory reconciles sources; Denial: hidden platform secrets are excluded; Failure: missing plane blocks finalization. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-31 |
| REQ-PRT-003 | Portable export | ERPX shall produce versioned open-format exports with schema, scope, counts, digests, provenance, and relationship manifests. | Customers need usable data. | Success: manifest verifies every file; Denial: unapproved recipient cannot download; Failure: incomplete package is not marked complete. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-31 |
| REQ-PRT-004 | Customer acknowledgement | ERPX shall obtain authorized acknowledgement of export scope, receipt, exceptions, and planned write fence. | Destructive stages require informed acceptance. | Success: exact manifest is acknowledged; Denial: changed package requires new acknowledgement; Failure: missing authority pauses exit. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-31 |
| REQ-PRT-005 | Write fence | ERPX shall apply a reversible scoped write fence before final delta and ownership termination. | Final export needs a stable source. | Success: protected commands reject after fence; Denial: unapproved fence is rejected; Failure: partial fence prevents final delta. | [ADR-003](../../adr/ADR-003-no-long-term-dual-writes.md) | E2E-31 |
| REQ-PRT-006 | Final delta | ERPX shall export and reconcile all accepted changes through the fenced source position. | Initial export may become stale. | Success: final cursor and counts reconcile; Denial: post-fence mutation cannot be hidden; Failure: gap blocks handoff. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-31 |
| REQ-PRT-007 | Revocation | ERPX shall revoke sessions, roles, service identities, agent work, provider routes, credentials, and downloads at the approved stage. | Exit must end authority comprehensively. | Success: next operation is denied; Denial: revoked identity cannot reactivate itself; Failure: unmatched provider revocation remains blocking work. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-31 |
| REQ-PRT-008 | Retention and hold | ERPX shall evaluate record class, jurisdiction, customer policy, legal hold, and evidence obligations before deletion. | Exit does not override retention law/policy. | Success: eligible data receives deletion schedule; Denial: active hold blocks purge; Failure: unresolved policy fails closed. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-31, E2E-42 |
| REQ-PRT-009 | Verified deletion | ERPX shall delete eligible primary, replica, object, cache, search, reference, comment, AI-retrieval, and provider copies with evidence. | Partial deletion is not customer exit. | Success: all targets attest deletion; Denial: held data is sealed rather than deleted; Failure: any mismatch prevents verified-complete status. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-31, E2E-68 |
| REQ-PRT-010 | Customer-hosted attestation | ERPX shall require a signed customer-hosted agent attestation for export, fence, revocation, retention, and deletion steps it executes. | Platform cannot directly observe every customer plane. | Success: attestation binds manifest and operation receipts; Denial: unverifiable statement is rejected; Failure: offline agent leaves exit incomplete. | [ADR-011](../../adr/ADR-011-customer-data-plane-agent.md) | E2E-31 |
