# Enterprise Work and Shared Services Requirements

These requirements define shared work, notification, and electronic-signature
outcomes. Business state remains with its source context.

## Enterprise work

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-WRK-001 | Enterprise Home | ERPX shall provide authorized `/work`, `/processes`, `/insights`, and `/modules` surfaces. | Users need one entry point without losing domain ownership. | Success: each surface shows scoped live projections; Denial: unauthorized modules are not discoverable; Failure: unavailable sources are named and not fabricated. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-21 |
| REQ-WRK-002 | Authorized command search | ERPX shall expose only commands the active principal may invoke in the active scope. | Search must not become an authority bypass. | Success: eligible commands resolve to typed actions; Denial: hidden commands reveal no metadata; Failure: stale authority makes the command unavailable. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-21 |
| REQ-WRK-003 | Federated work projection | ERPX shall compose approvals, tasks, exceptions, incidents, mentions, and agent assignments as a disposable read model. | One inbox must not own source work. | Success: every row names source and freshness; Denial: inaccessible rows and counts are absent; Failure: one source failure is isolated and disclosed. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-21, E2E-45 |
| REQ-WRK-004 | Source-owned lifecycle | ERPX shall route every work mutation to the bounded context that owns the work item. | Federated work must preserve write authority. | Success: typed source command returns a receipt; Denial: BFF-side mutation is rejected; Failure: partial source failure leaves the projection unchanged until reconciliation. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-21 |
| REQ-WRK-005 | Work detail link | ERPX shall give every work row an authorized stable reference to its source record. | Users need durable deep links across channels. | Success: the reference resolves to the canonical route; Denial: unauthorized resolution is indistinguishable from absence; Failure: a moved route resolves after descriptor refresh. | [FSD-005](decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-50, E2E-68 |
| REQ-WRK-006 | Work filters | ERPX shall filter work by source domain, kind, stage, severity, assignee, scope, and due state. | Large enterprises need bounded queues. | Success: filters return authorized matches with stable paging; Denial: facets do not disclose hidden counts; Failure: invalid cursors are rejected without fallback leakage. | [Current work model](../crates/ui/src/work.rs) | E2E-21 |
| REQ-WRK-007 | Work SLA evidence | ERPX shall derive due and escalation state from versioned source policy and accepted timestamps. | SLA labels must be reproducible. | Success: due state reconciles to policy; Denial: users cannot edit a derived SLA label; Failure: missing policy shows unknown rather than guessed status. | [Current work model](../crates/ui/src/work.rs) | E2E-21 |
| REQ-WRK-008 | Assignment command | ERPX shall change human ownership only through an explicit typed Assign command. | Mentions must not silently change accountability. | Success: authorized assignment records actor and receipt; Denial: a comment mention leaves assignee unchanged; Failure: stale item version rejects reassignment. | [FSD-005](decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-45, E2E-59 |
| REQ-WRK-009 | Cross-domain exceptions | ERPX shall let Operations own only cross-domain exception coordination and not source business state. | Operational visibility must not collapse contexts. | Success: exception links immutable source facts; Denial: Operations cannot edit source order or journal; Failure: source correction reconciles without duplicate exception. | [ADR-008](../adr/ADR-008-operations-boundaries.md) | E2E-19, E2E-21 |
| REQ-WRK-010 | Projection freshness | ERPX shall display work-source freshness and make stale work read-only when command safety cannot be proved. | Users must know when a queue lags. | Success: fresh sources remain actionable; Denial: stale commands are disabled; Failure: unavailable freshness produces an explicit degraded state. | [ADR-002](../adr/ADR-002-cqrs.md) | E2E-16, E2E-21 |
| REQ-WRK-011 | Work evidence | ERPX shall preserve correlation from work projection through source command receipt and audit. | Reviewers need an end-to-end chain. | Success: a work action resolves to source receipt; Denial: unrelated actors cannot inspect evidence; Failure: missing links block sign-off. | [FSD-001](decisions/FSD-001-baseline-and-governance.md) | E2E-21 |
| REQ-WRK-012 | AI and collaboration workspace | ERPX shall provide `/ai` for authorized thread and agent work without removing embedded domain experiences. | Collaboration needs a governed home and local context. | Success: module and embedded surfaces show the same source facts; Denial: unentitled users cannot discover it; Failure: module outage does not remove source-domain records. | [FSD-006](decisions/FSD-006-clean-rebuild-architecture-and-cutover.md) | E2E-45, E2E-60 |

## Notifications

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-NTF-001 | Authoritative inbox | ERPX shall use the in-app inbox as notification authority. | Optional channels cannot be the only evidence. | Success: every notice has durable inbox state; Denial: adapter access cannot mutate business state; Failure: provider outage retains retryable inbox facts. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-22 |
| REQ-NTF-002 | Optional adapters | ERPX shall deliver Email, SMS, Push, and Webhook only through governed optional adapters. | Customers choose channels and providers. | Success: enabled adapters receive bounded payloads; Denial: disabled channels receive nothing; Failure: adapter error records a redacted attempt. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-22 |
| REQ-NTF-003 | Deduplication | ERPX shall deduplicate delivery by notification, recipient, channel, and delivery version. | Retries must not spam recipients. | Success: repeated work creates one logical delivery; Denial: changed payload cannot reuse an old key; Failure: uncertain outcome reconciles before resend. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-22, E2E-55 |
| REQ-NTF-004 | Preferences | ERPX shall honor per-user channel, quiet-hour, mute, snooze, and follow preferences within mandatory-policy limits. | Users need control without defeating regulated notices. | Success: permitted preferences affect future delivery; Denial: mandatory delivery cannot be silently muted; Failure: invalid timezone falls back visibly and safely. | [FSD-005](decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-45, E2E-55 |
| REQ-NTF-005 | Acknowledgement | ERPX shall record unread, read, acknowledged, and action-required states separately. | Reading is not the same as accepting responsibility. | Success: state transitions are attributable; Denial: recipients cannot acknowledge for another principal; Failure: concurrent acknowledgements remain idempotent. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-22, E2E-45 |
| REQ-NTF-006 | Safe previews | ERPX shall exclude content a recipient cannot currently read from notification previews. | Notifications must not leak protected records. | Success: authorized previews show bounded context; Denial: revoked access shows a neutral notice; Failure: authorization uncertainty suppresses the preview. | [FSD-005](decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-49, E2E-51 |
| REQ-NTF-007 | Group fan-out | ERPX shall snapshot authorized human-group recipients and group version at send time. | Historical mention evidence must not drift with membership. | Success: only the snapshot receives delivery; Denial: later members receive no historical notification; Failure: excessive fan-out requires broadcast authority or rejection. | [FSD-005](decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-55 |
| REQ-NTF-008 | Delivery reconciliation | ERPX shall reconcile provider callbacks without treating provider acceptance as business completion. | Channel status and business outcome differ. | Success: callback advances delivery evidence once; Denial: unverified callbacks are rejected; Failure: out-of-order callbacks converge deterministically. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-22 |
| REQ-NTF-009 | Agent badges | ERPX shall signal agent completion, failure, or input-needed with badges and inbox events without opening the AI panel. | Background agents must not steal focus. | Success: state is announced accessibly; Denial: unrelated users see no badge; Failure: missed realtime events recover from inbox state. | [FSD-005](decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-60, E2E-63 |
| REQ-NTF-010 | Notification audit | ERPX shall retain redacted creation, fan-out, attempt, acknowledgement, withdrawal, and suppression evidence. | Disputes require a complete delivery chain. | Success: auditors correlate events without message secrets; Denial: ordinary users cannot inspect other recipients; Failure: missing evidence blocks acceptance. | [FSD-001](decisions/FSD-001-baseline-and-governance.md) | E2E-22, E2E-55 |

## Electronic signatures

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-ESG-001 | Internal approval receipt | ERPX shall bind an internal approval receipt to the exact document digest and signer authority. | A signature must identify what was accepted. | Success: valid receipt resolves signer, digest, time, and scope; Denial: changed content invalidates the receipt; Failure: unavailable verification blocks completion. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-27 |
| REQ-ESG-002 | Provider envelope | ERPX shall orchestrate provider-backed envelopes through a versioned adapter. | Providers must not own ERPX business state. | Success: envelope pins document and recipients; Denial: unapproved provider is rejected; Failure: provider outage leaves a resumable ERPX state. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-27 |
| REQ-ESG-003 | Signer order | ERPX shall enforce configured serial, parallel, or conditional signer order. | Document authority depends on the intended sequence. | Success: eligible signer advances the envelope; Denial: out-of-order signature is rejected; Failure: expired signer authority pauses the workflow. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-27 |
| REQ-ESG-004 | External signer realm | ERPX shall use a one-time participant/signatory realm without merging it into workforce or customer credentials. | Temporary signers need bounded access. | Success: token opens only the named envelope; Denial: email equality grants no other identity; Failure: expired or revoked token reveals no document. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-27 |
| REQ-ESG-005 | Callback integrity | ERPX shall authenticate, deduplicate, order, and reconcile provider callbacks. | Callback replay must not duplicate completion. | Success: verified callback advances once; Denial: invalid signature is rejected and audited; Failure: gaps trigger reconciliation. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-27 |
| REQ-ESG-006 | Evidence package | ERPX shall preserve document digest, signer order, identity evidence, provider events, timestamps, and final verification. | Legal review needs a portable record. | Success: authorized export verifies the package; Denial: unrelated users cannot download it; Failure: incomplete evidence prevents a verified label. | [FSD-002](decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-27 |
| REQ-ESG-007 | Retention and hold | ERPX shall apply record retention, jurisdiction policy, and legal hold to signature evidence. | Signature evidence must follow its record. | Success: eligible evidence is deleted with proof; Denial: active hold blocks deletion; Failure: unresolved policy fails closed. | [FSD-004](decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-12, E2E-27 |
| REQ-ESG-008 | Legal validity boundary | ERPX shall describe technical signature evidence without claiming automatic legal validity. | Legal effect depends on jurisdiction and use case. | Success: certified packs name their scope; Denial: uncertified configuration cannot show a compliance claim; Failure: changed regulation requires recertification. | [FSD-004](decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-27, E2E-42 |
