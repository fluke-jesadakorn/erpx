# Field Service Requirements

Field Service owns service demand, orders, appointments, visits, execution
evidence, and completion handoffs. Operations coordinates dispatch; Inventory,
Assets, Customer, Sales, and Finance retain source authority.

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-FSV-001 | Service demand | ERPX shall create a source-linked service demand from customer, asset, maintenance, warranty, project, or support facts. | Demand origin must remain explainable. | Success: one demand links source/version; Denial: unscoped source is rejected; Failure: duplicate event maps to one demand. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-002 | Service-order lifecycle | ERPX shall govern draft, approval, release, dispatch, travel, onsite, paused, completed, cancelled, closed, and archived states. | Field execution needs explicit control. | Success: valid transition records receipt; Denial: invalid order is rejected; Failure: interrupted visit remains resumable online. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-003 | Entitlement and warranty | ERPX shall validate service entitlement, contract, warranty, and charge policy before release. | Coverage affects billing. | Success: order pins accepted coverage; Denial: unsupported free service is rejected; Failure: unavailable authority holds release. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-004 | Skill eligibility | ERPX shall match technicians using authorized skill, role, certification-record, scope, and availability facts. | Dispatch must be safe and privacy-bounded. | Success: eligible technician is assignable; Denial: missing required skill blocks assignment; Failure: stale qualification flags review. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-005 | Appointment | ERPX shall govern proposed, offered, confirmed, rescheduled, missed, and cancelled appointments. | Customer commitment must be clear. | Success: both sides receive confirmed window; Denial: unauthorized reschedule is rejected; Failure: conflict returns alternatives. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-006 | Dispatch coordination | ERPX shall coordinate dispatch through Operations without transferring service-order ownership. | Dispatch is cross-domain work. | Success: accepted assignment updates route/work queue; Denial: Operations cannot rewrite service evidence; Failure: rejected dispatch returns reason. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-007 | Visit start | ERPX shall start a visit only for an authorized technician, released order, and valid appointment or override. | Onsite work requires admission. | Success: start records time/scope; Denial: wrong technician/order is rejected; Failure: location/time uncertainty requires reasoned override. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-008 | Online mutation | ERPX shall require online authority for field-service business mutations. | Baseline offline policy is read-only. | Success: online command returns receipt; Denial: offline device cannot queue a mutation draft; Failure: network loss preserves unsent UI state without claiming success. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-009 | Work evidence | ERPX shall record time, findings, measurements, actions, and bounded attachments by visit. | Completion needs defensible evidence. | Success: evidence links technician/order/time; Denial: unsafe attachment is quarantined; Failure: missing required evidence blocks completion. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-010 | Parts reservation | ERPX shall request Inventory reservation for planned field parts. | Field Service does not own stock. | Success: accepted reservation binds order/location; Denial: unavailable part cannot be assumed; Failure: timeout reconciles idempotently. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-011 | Parts consumption | ERPX shall accept authoritative Inventory issue/return facts for the visit. | Billing and stock require exact movement. | Success: consumption posts once; Denial: technician cannot fabricate stock; Failure: mismatch creates correction work. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-012 | Asset update handoff | ERPX shall propose meter, condition, component, and maintenance changes to Assets or Maintenance. | Field observations do not grant asset mutation authority. | Success: owner accepts exact evidence; Denial: Field Service cannot overwrite asset state; Failure: rejection remains linked. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-013 | Customer sign-off | ERPX shall bind customer sign-off or reasoned refusal to exact visit completion evidence. | Completion disputes require a pinned record. | Success: authorized signer acknowledges digest; Denial: expired signer token is rejected; Failure: refusal routes exception without fabricated signature. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-014 | Completion | ERPX shall complete a visit only after required work, evidence, parts, safety, and sign-off gates. | Downstream billing depends on valid completion. | Success: completed visit publishes one fact; Denial: missing gate blocks completion; Failure: correction uses explicit reversal. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-015 | Billing handoff | ERPX shall hand approved labor, travel, parts, fee, and coverage facts to Sales or Finance. | Field Service cannot invoice. | Success: accepted facts create one billing candidate; Denial: technician cannot post invoice; Failure: rejected lines return reasons. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-016 | Follow-up | ERPX shall create governed follow-up demand for unresolved findings without reopening closed evidence silently. | Additional work needs a new accountable lifecycle. | Success: new demand links prior visit; Denial: duplicate follow-up is rejected; Failure: assignment absence enters work queue. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-017 | Service close | ERPX shall close only after visit, inventory, asset, customer, billing, and exception reconciliation. | Close certifies completeness. | Success: all gates pass once; Denial: unresolved exception blocks close; Failure: missing receipt is retrievable. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
| REQ-FSV-018 | Service evidence | ERPX shall expose authorized order-to-billing provenance and freshness. | Customer and audit need one trace. | Success: timeline links every source receipt; Denial: internal notes and hidden locations are redacted; Failure: missing source is explicit. | [FSD-003](../decisions/FSD-003-enterprise-suite-expansion.md) | E2E-40 |
