# Reference Routing and Collaboration Data Requirements

The Item Directory resolves opaque references and builds authorized backlinks.
It is a disposable projection and never becomes source business authority.

## First-class record and route catalog

Every row declares lifecycle aggregate roots that receive an owner-issued
`RecordRef.id`. The listed routes are singular target detail templates owned
by the context; `/reference/item/:id` remains the stable copied-link resolver. A
route template may change through a versioned descriptor without changing the
opaque reference. The authority column identifies the present repository
source for ownership and the accepted future-state decision governing routes.
Every route in this catalog is additionally governed by
[FSD-006](../decisions/FSD-006-clean-rebuild-architecture-and-cutover.md).

Internal lines, individual domain events, outbox rows, and projection
checkpoints are not first-class records. A field, block, page, region, line, or
record version is addressed only by an anchored sub-reference through its
cataloged parent record, source revision, locator, and digest.

## Normative requirements

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-REF-001 | First-class boundary | ERPX shall assign reference identity only to lifecycle aggregate roots declared in the owner catalog. | Internal rows must not become accidental records. | Success: cataloged aggregate gets descriptor; Denial: line/event/checkpoint is rejected; Failure: unknown ownership blocks registration. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-50, E2E-68 |
| REQ-REF-002 | Opaque global reference | ERPX shall give each first-class record a non-enumerable globally unique reference ID distinct from its business code. | Routes must not leak predictable identifiers. | Success: ID remains stable across rename/move; Denial: business code cannot substitute at resolver boundary; Failure: collision prevents creation. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-50, E2E-51 |
| REQ-REF-003 | Owner-issued identity | ERPX shall let the authoritative context issue the reference ID in the same accepted record lifecycle. | No central synchronous write dependency is desired. | Success: accepted aggregate emits descriptor; Denial: Item Directory cannot issue business state; Failure: outbox retry preserves one ID. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-68 |
| REQ-REF-004 | Item descriptor | ERPX shall publish owner, type, opaque ID, scope, canonical route, lifecycle, authorization policy, descriptor version, and source position. | Resolver needs bounded facts. | Success: complete descriptor projects; Denial: sensitive title/body is not required; Failure: malformed descriptor is quarantined. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-50, E2E-68 |
| REQ-REF-005 | Canonical domain route | ERPX shall require each record type to declare its owner-controlled canonical detail-route template. | Domains retain experience ownership. | Success: resolver redirects to current route; Denial: uncataloged route cannot publish; Failure: route change creates new descriptor version. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-50, E2E-68 |
| REQ-REF-006 | Stable resolver | ERPX shall expose `/reference/item/:id` as an authorized resolver rather than a business detail page. | Copied links must survive route changes. | Success: authorized request returns a no-store redirect to the current singular route; Denial: absent/denied results are indistinguishable; Failure: stale index returns neutral unavailable state. | [FSD-006](../decisions/FSD-006-clean-rebuild-architecture-and-cutover.md) | E2E-50, E2E-51 |
| REQ-REF-007 | Reauthorization | ERPX shall re-evaluate realm, role, hierarchy, scope, record, and field authority on every resolver/detail open. | Stored references are not access grants. | Success: current authority opens record; Denial: revoked user sees neutral result; Failure: policy outage fails closed. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-49, E2E-51 |
| REQ-REF-008 | Restricted placeholder | ERPX shall render unauthorized embedded references as `Restricted item` without type, title, business ID, or hidden count. | Conversation structure can remain without leakage. | Success: placeholder preserves sentence position; Denial: hover/API reveals nothing more; Failure: uncertain access uses placeholder. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-51 |
| REQ-REF-009 | Record move | ERPX shall preserve opaque identity when a record changes canonical route or permitted organizational scope. | References must not break. | Success: new descriptor redirects correctly; Denial: unauthorized move is rejected; Failure: transition window shows safe unavailable state. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-68 |
| REQ-REF-010 | Record retirement | ERPX shall project archived, retired, merged, superseded, and tombstoned outcomes without reusing identity. | Historical links need truthful state. | Success: authorized resolver shows governed outcome; Denial: retired ID cannot identify new record; Failure: missing successor link remains explicit. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-50, E2E-68 |
| REQ-REF-011 | Typed relation | ERPX shall store versioned `relates-to`, `blocks`, `depends-on`, `implements`, `supersedes`, `derived-from`, and `evidence-for` relations plus only approved domain-specific types, with inverse, endpoints, source version, actor, and effective state. | Relationships need stable semantics. | Success: valid relation appears at both endpoints; Denial: arbitrary unregistered type is rejected; Failure: partial inverse projection rebuilds. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-52 |
| REQ-REF-012 | Relation effect boundary | ERPX shall require typed owner-context commands and required acceptance for relations that affect lifecycle, schedule, or authority. | A link alone must not mutate records. | Success: accepted blocking relation has receipts; Denial: comment `#` token is non-mutating; Failure: one-sided failure reconciles. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-59, E2E-65 |
| REQ-REF-013 | Authorized backlinks | ERPX shall derive incoming and outgoing backlinks only after viewer authorization at both endpoints. | Backlinks can expose hidden work. | Success: viewer sees permitted links; Denial: hidden links and counts are absent; Failure: unavailable policy suppresses backlink. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-52 |
| REQ-REF-014 | Anchor sub-reference | ERPX shall represent line, field, block, page, or region references through a parent record, source revision, locator, and digest. | Sub-record review needs stable context without new authority. | Success: locator resolves exact version; Denial: parent denial hides anchor; Failure: changed source marks orphaned. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-54 |
| REQ-REF-015 | Projection ordering | ERPX shall apply descriptor and relation events in owner-key order with duplicate suppression and gap detection. | Directory consistency must be deterministic. | Success: contiguous cursor advances; Denial: out-of-order gap is not published; Failure: replay resumes from checkpoint. | [ADR-002](../../adr/ADR-002-cqrs.md) | E2E-68 |
| REQ-REF-016 | Projection rebuild | ERPX shall clear and rebuild Item Directory and backlink projections from owner events without mutating sources. | Derived routing must be recoverable. | Success: rebuild reaches same descriptors; Denial: projector has no source write path; Failure: gap prevents cutover to rebuilt index. | [ADR-002](../../adr/ADR-002-cqrs.md) | E2E-16, E2E-68 |
| REQ-REF-017 | Deletion propagation | ERPX shall propagate deletion, erasure, merge, access revocation, and legal-hold effects to resolver, embeds, search, backlinks, caches, and AI retrieval. | Copies must follow source governance. | Success: bounded propagation removes/redacts views; Denial: stale copy cannot remain actionable; Failure: unresolved propagation blocks verified deletion. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-23, E2E-31, E2E-68 |
| REQ-REF-018 | Resolver audit | ERPX shall audit sensitive resolution purpose, principal, scope, outcome, correlation, and redirect class without storing secrets. | Cross-domain navigation needs oversight. | Success: authorized audit explains access; Denial: ordinary users cannot inspect others; Failure: missing purpose blocks regulated detail access. | [ADR-005](../../adr/ADR-005-global-read-auditing.md) | E2E-50, E2E-51 |
