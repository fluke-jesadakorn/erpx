# Jurisdiction and Sector Pack Requirements

The global core is law-neutral. Packs are versioned overlays requiring official
sources, Privacy/Legal review, and external certification for their stated
scope. They do not create automatic legal-compliance claims.

## Pack governance

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-REG-001 | Pack identity | ERPX shall version every jurisdiction/sector pack with scope, authority, effective dates, owner, certifier, and supersession. | Regulations change over time. | Success: transaction pins effective pack; Denial: draft/expired pack cannot claim certification; Failure: overlap blocks activation. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-41, E2E-42 |
| REQ-REG-002 | Official source | ERPX shall map every regulated rule to an official source-register entry and retrieved-version evidence. | Requirements need authoritative origin. | Success: rule resolves source/version; Denial: unsupported interpretation cannot activate; Failure: inaccessible source triggers Legal review. | [Source register](../references/regulatory-source-register.md) | E2E-41, E2E-42 |
| REQ-REG-003 | External certification | ERPX shall require external Legal/Regulatory certification before displaying a compliance claim. | Product tests do not prove legal validity. | Success: claim names scope/version/certifier; Denial: uncertified pack shows no claim; Failure: material change invalidates certification. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-41, E2E-42 |
| REQ-REG-004 | Policy data | ERPX shall store changing rates, forms, thresholds, deadlines, and codes as versioned governed policy data. | Volatile rules must not be hard-coded. | Success: effective value is reproducible; Denial: overlapping value is rejected; Failure: missing value fails closed. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-41 |
| REQ-REG-005 | Applicability | ERPX shall resolve jurisdiction and sector applicability from organization, transaction, person, period, and policy facts. | Not every rule applies everywhere. | Success: decision records inputs; Denial: user cannot self-declare exemption without authority; Failure: ambiguity routes review. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-41, E2E-44 |
| REQ-REG-006 | Evidence package | ERPX shall retain calculation, source, policy, approvals, submissions, responses, corrections, and certification evidence. | Regulators and auditors need provenance. | Success: authorized package verifies; Denial: unrelated users cannot inspect it; Failure: evidence gap blocks certified outcome. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-41, E2E-44 |
| REQ-REG-007 | Pack rollback | ERPX shall support preview, cohort activation, rollback, and correction for a pack version without rewriting accepted history. | Regulatory changes can be wrong or delayed. | Success: rollback restores prior future-effective policy; Denial: posted facts are not silently recalculated; Failure: affected records enter correction workflow. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-30, E2E-41 |
| REQ-REG-008 | Law-neutral fallback | ERPX shall not silently apply another jurisdiction's rules when no certified pack resolves. | Convenience fallback can create legal error. | Success: global business record remains unclassified/non-certified; Denial: regulated operation is blocked where policy requires; Failure: missing pack is explicit. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-41, E2E-42 |

## Thailand pack

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-THA-001 | Thailand pack activation | ERPX shall activate the Thailand pack only for explicitly scoped Thai entities and effective periods. | Pack effects must be bounded. | Success: eligible entity resolves version; Denial: foreign/unscoped entity is excluded; Failure: ambiguous establishment blocks regulated processing. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-41 |
| REQ-THA-002 | Tax identifiers | ERPX shall validate, classify, protect, and effective-date Thai tax identifiers without inferring identity solely from them. | Identifiers are sensitive and reusable. | Success: authorized use resolves exact entity; Denial: unauthorized search is blocked; Failure: conflict requires governed resolution. | [Thai Revenue Department](https://www.rd.go.th/65244.html) | E2E-41 |
| REQ-THA-003 | Tax document type | ERPX shall determine Thai tax-document type from certified policy and source transaction facts. | Document semantics vary. | Success: eligible invoice pins document rule; Denial: user cannot force unsupported type; Failure: unresolved rule blocks issuance. | [Thai Revenue Department](https://www.rd.go.th/65244.html) | E2E-41 |
| REQ-THA-004 | E-tax document | ERPX shall create a versioned, digest-bound Thai e-tax document from posted Finance facts. | External tax documents must match accounting. | Success: generated document reconciles amounts; Denial: unposted source cannot issue; Failure: rendering/signing error leaves no issued status. | [e-Tax system](https://etax.rd.go.th/) | E2E-41 |
| REQ-THA-005 | E-tax exchange | ERPX shall exchange Thai e-tax documents through a certified adapter and reconcile acknowledgements, rejection, cancellation, and correction. | Submission is not acceptance. | Success: accepted response links document; Denial: unverified callback is rejected; Failure: uncertain status enters reconciliation. | [e-Tax system](https://etax.rd.go.th/) | E2E-41 |
| REQ-THA-006 | Withholding rule | ERPX shall calculate withholding from versioned certified policy and exact payment/payee facts. | Rates and forms change. | Success: calculation pins source entry; Denial: hard-coded override is rejected; Failure: unresolved category blocks posting. | [Withholding tax](https://www.rd.go.th/27862.html) | E2E-41 |
| REQ-THA-007 | Withholding evidence | ERPX shall issue and retain withholding document, payment linkage, submission, correction, and recipient evidence. | Finance needs a complete chain. | Success: amounts reconcile to source; Denial: unrelated recipient is denied; Failure: correction supersedes without rewriting original. | [Withholding tax](https://www.rd.go.th/27862.html) | E2E-41 |
| REQ-THA-008 | Personal tax | ERPX shall calculate Thai employee tax from certified effective policy and finalized payroll facts. | Payroll tax must be reproducible. | Success: employee result reconciles run; Denial: unauthorized override is rejected; Failure: missing policy blocks finalization. | [Personal income tax](https://www.rd.go.th/27860.html) | E2E-41 |
| REQ-THA-009 | Social security | ERPX shall calculate and reconcile Thai social-security contribution facts from versioned policy. | Contribution limits and rules change. | Success: employee/employer amounts reconcile; Denial: unsupported exemption is rejected; Failure: provider mismatch creates work. | [Social Security Office](https://catalog.sso.go.th/th/organization/https-www-sso-go-th-wpr-main) | E2E-41 |
| REQ-THA-010 | Thailand privacy annex | ERPX shall apply a versioned Thailand privacy annex for purpose, notice, rights, DPO, retention, breach, and evidence workflows. | Global controls need local interpretation. | Success: scoped request follows certified annex; Denial: unverified requester is rejected; Failure: ambiguity escalates to Privacy/Legal. | [PDPC DPO registration](https://dpo.pdpc.or.th/registration), [GPPC privacy policy](https://gppc.pdpc.or.th/privacy-policy/) | E2E-42 |
| REQ-THA-011 | Privacy request | ERPX shall record identity verification, scope, search, decision, response, exceptions, and completion evidence for Thai privacy requests. | Rights handling must be auditable. | Success: eligible request completes within configured policy; Denial: invalid/exempt request records lawful reason; Failure: source gap remains escalated. | [GPPC operational training](https://gppc.pdpc.or.th/operational-training-for-platform-installation-and-usage/) | E2E-42 |
| REQ-THA-012 | Banking overlay | ERPX shall provide back-office classification, IT-risk controls, report extracts, integrations, and certification evidence for regulated banking customers. | Scope excludes core banking. | Success: certified overlay applies to back-office records; Denial: core banking transaction commands are unavailable; Failure: source change triggers recertification. | [BOT risk reporting](https://www.bot.or.th/th/our-services/data-acquisition-publication/report-document-financial-institution/riskmgt-fi.html), [BOT IT risk](https://www.bot.or.th/th/our-roles/payment-systems/information-technology-risk-supervision.html) | E2E-43 |
| REQ-THA-013 | Insurance overlay | ERPX shall provide back-office classification, IT-risk controls, extracts, integrations, and certification evidence for insurance customers. | Scope excludes policy/claims administration. | Success: certified controls apply; Denial: claims adjudication is unavailable; Failure: regulatory change invalidates affected certification. | [OIC announcement](https://oiceservice.oic.or.th/document/Law/file/11389/11389_3956dee0c06add1ae53b1e3ce8a3e3c4.pdf) | E2E-44 |
| REQ-THA-014 | Healthcare overlay | ERPX shall provide back-office classification, security controls, extracts, integrations, and certification evidence for healthcare customers. | Scope excludes clinical systems. | Success: certified controls apply to ERP records; Denial: clinical diagnosis/treatment commands are unavailable; Failure: policy change triggers review. | [MOPH security policy](https://ict.moph.go.th/th/extension/1083) | E2E-44 |
