# AI Assistance and Controlled Extensions Requirements

AI is assistive and governed. Extensions are allow-listed typed configuration,
not arbitrary code, SQL, or a public developer platform.

## Assistive AI

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-AI-001 | No business authority | ERPX shall treat AI output as proposal, summary, explanation, draft, or typed action proposal only. | Models cannot hold enterprise authority. | Success: human/policy decides mutation; Denial: model output cannot post state directly; Failure: ambiguous action remains non-mutating. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-28, E2E-59 |
| REQ-AI-002 | Local-first default | ERPX shall default to Ollama, local, or customer-hosted approved models. | Customers need a data-local option. | Success: eligible local model is selected; Denial: external egress requires opt-in; Failure: unavailable local model is explicit. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-28, E2E-61 |
| REQ-AI-003 | Approved external provider | ERPX shall permit external AI only after provider, model, use case, DPA, residency, and classification approval. | External models introduce data egress. | Success: matching policy permits bounded payload; Denial: unmatched use is blocked; Failure: expired approval fails closed. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-61 |
| REQ-AI-004 | Scoped retrieval | ERPX shall retrieve only records, comments, and fields authorized for the effective principal and provider. | RAG must not widen access. | Success: citations resolve authorized sources; Denial: hidden content/snippets are absent; Failure: authorization uncertainty returns no protected context. | [FSD-005](../decisions/FSD-005-collaboration-references-and-agents.md) | E2E-23, E2E-61 |
| REQ-AI-005 | Source provenance | ERPX shall attach source references, versions, freshness, and retrieval cutoff to factual AI output. | Users need verifiable answers. | Success: cited fact opens authorized source; Denial: hidden source is not described; Failure: unsupported fact is labeled uncertain. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-28 |
| REQ-AI-006 | Prompt-injection defense | ERPX shall separate instructions, data, tools, and untrusted content and enforce tool allow-lists outside the model. | Retrieved content can be adversarial. | Success: untrusted instructions cannot change policy; Denial: disallowed tool call is blocked; Failure: detected attack quarantines action. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-28, E2E-61 |
| REQ-AI-007 | Data-loss prevention | ERPX shall apply secret, classification, residency, purpose, and minimization controls before provider transmission. | User access alone does not authorize every egress. | Success: allowed payload has manifest; Denial: secret/prohibited field is removed or blocked; Failure: scanner uncertainty fails closed. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-61 |
| REQ-AI-008 | Evaluation evidence | ERPX shall require versioned quality, safety, authorization, leakage, and failure evaluations before agent/model approval. | Models vary across versions. | Success: passing evidence pins version; Denial: missing eval prevents approval; Failure: regression suspends affected use. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-28, E2E-46 |
| REQ-AI-009 | Human-readable uncertainty | ERPX shall disclose material uncertainty, missing sources, stale context, and model limitations. | Confident presentation can mislead. | Success: limitations accompany output; Denial: unsupported compliance/decision claim is blocked; Failure: missing source prevents definitive label. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-28 |
| REQ-AI-010 | Model change control | ERPX shall approve model/provider replacement as a new governed version with compatibility and rollback evidence. | Silent model changes break reproducibility. | Success: cohort receives pinned version; Denial: provider alias cannot drift silently; Failure: rollback restores prior approved version. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-30, E2E-46 |
| REQ-AI-011 | AI incident evidence | ERPX shall preserve redacted invocation, policy, model, tool, output, report, and remediation evidence for AI incidents. | Harmful outputs need investigation. | Success: authorized investigator reconstructs event; Denial: ordinary logs omit prompts/secrets; Failure: evidence gap escalates. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-67 |
| REQ-AI-012 | No certification claim | ERPX shall not claim AI correctness, regulatory compliance, or autonomous approval without external certification and accepted scope. | AI evidence is not legal authority. | Success: claims name certification scope; Denial: uncertified badge/text is unavailable; Failure: changed model invalidates prior certification. | [FSD-004](../decisions/FSD-004-thailand-and-sector-overlays.md) | E2E-28, E2E-42 |

## Controlled extensions

| ID | Title | Normative requirement | Rationale | Acceptance paths | Source | Scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-XTN-001 | Allow-listed extension | ERPX shall permit only registered typed custom fields, forms, and workflow templates. | Customers need bounded customization. | Success: approved type is configurable; Denial: arbitrary code/SQL is rejected; Failure: unknown extension renders read-only fallback. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-29 |
| REQ-XTN-002 | Extension ownership | ERPX shall assign owner, scope, purpose, classification, and affected context to every extension. | Custom data still needs governance. | Success: metadata is complete; Denial: ownerless extension cannot activate; Failure: owner departure triggers review. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-29 |
| REQ-XTN-003 | Versioned schema | ERPX shall version extension schema, validation, display, indexing, import/export, and default semantics. | Change must be migratable. | Success: records retain schema version; Denial: incompatible edit cannot reuse version; Failure: migration preview exposes affected records. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-29 |
| REQ-XTN-004 | Authority ceiling | ERPX shall prevent an extension from overriding core ownership, authorization, SoD, audit, retention, or command invariants. | Customization cannot weaken controls. | Success: compatible extension works within source command; Denial: privilege override is rejected; Failure: uncertain conflict blocks activation. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-29 |
| REQ-XTN-005 | Workflow mapping | ERPX shall map custom workflow states to governed core states where shared work/reporting requires it. | Cross-domain views need common semantics. | Success: every active state maps once; Denial: unmapped active state cannot publish; Failure: changed map requires migration. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-29, E2E-64 |
| REQ-XTN-006 | Search and reporting | ERPX shall index/report extension fields only when classification, authorization, and semantic metadata permit. | Custom fields can contain restricted data. | Success: eligible field participates with provenance; Denial: hidden field leaves no facet/snippet; Failure: missing classification excludes it. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-23, E2E-24, E2E-29 |
| REQ-XTN-007 | Retirement | ERPX shall retire an extension through dependency inventory, export, migration, archive, and rollback policy. | Removing fields can destroy records. | Success: governed plan preserves data; Denial: active dependency blocks destructive removal; Failure: migration rollback restores prior version. | [FSD-002](../decisions/FSD-002-shared-platform-capabilities.md) | E2E-29 |
| REQ-XTN-008 | Extension audit | ERPX shall preserve definition, approval, publication, use, migration, and retirement evidence. | Customer-specific behavior must be explainable. | Success: authorized reviewer traces effective version; Denial: protected values are redacted; Failure: missing evidence prevents activation. | [FSD-001](../decisions/FSD-001-baseline-and-governance.md) | E2E-29 |
