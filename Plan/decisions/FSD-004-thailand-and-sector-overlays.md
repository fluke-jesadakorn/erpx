# FSD-004 — Thailand and Regulated-Sector Overlays

- Status: Accepted
- Owner: Privacy and Legal
- Effective baseline: 1.0.0
- Supersedes: none

## Decision

The global core remains law-neutral. Thailand is the first jurisdiction pack
and covers tax, payroll, withholding, e-Tax, privacy, and versioned regulatory
sources. Banking, insurance, and healthcare receive back-office control,
classification, reporting, integration, and certification overlays; ERPX does
not become a core banking, insurance policy/claims, or clinical system.

Changing rates, forms, durations, and rules are versioned policy data rather
than hard-coded requirements. External Legal/Regulatory certification is
required before a compliance claim.
