# Licensing and provenance

## Current project grant

The project owner has authorized Apache-2.0 for ERPX-owned current source code and documentation as of 2026-10-27. The [license text](LICENSE) contains the terms; [NOTICE](NOTICE) records known attribution. Application workspaces remain `private: true` to prevent accidental package publication. That setting does not restrict rights granted by the license.

This grant covers only material the project has the right to license. Third-party code, fonts and other separately licensed material retain their notices and terms. A project-level license is not a claim that all dependencies, media, screenshots or historical files have already been reviewed.

## Historical source

The archived Rust workspace declared `LicenseRef-Proprietary`. Its original notices and dated evidence remain unchanged. The current Apache-2.0 grant must not be presented as automatically relicensing every historical snapshot or another contributor's work.

Before historical source is reused or retained in a public release, record its origin, rights holder, applicable license and any additional grant in the [provenance register](docs/community/provenance.json). A historical grant must identify the material and an authorized rights holder. Unresolved third-party rights remain unresolved; a new implementation language does not resolve them.

## Dependencies, assets and marks

- Preserve existing vendor and font licenses. Redistribution must include the notices required by each dependency's license.
- Record generated media, photographs, videos, screenshots and other non-code assets separately. Known generation or acquisition history is evidence of origin, not a complete rights clearance.
- The Apache-2.0 license does not grant a general right to use a contributor's trademarks. Do not imply that a modified distribution is endorsed by the ERPX maintainers.
- Produce source and runtime dependency inventories for each release. Image dependencies and their notices join that inventory when container releases are implemented.

The register is a starting inventory and explicitly remains incomplete. Its presence is not a public-release approval.

## Contributions

Submit contributions under the applicable project or file license and retain upstream notices. Use a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Disclose copied or adapted source, generated assets and material external assistance so reviewers can check provenance. Do not submit material you cannot redistribute under compatible terms.

## Public-history preparation

Keep the original repository and immutable evidence in a private, independently restorable backup. Prepare any necessary secret or personal-data redaction in an isolated mirror after the implementation PRs merge. Preserve authorship, timestamps and commit relationships, and record old-to-new commit/ref mappings. Rewritten history has new hashes and cannot retain valid original commit or tag signatures.

Public-history preparation, repository visibility and artifact publication are separate from committing and merging development source. See the [open-source preparation guide](docs/community/open-source.md) for the review gates. No history rewrite or publication has been performed by adding this policy.
